Registracija redakcije i siguran pristupNewsroom registration and secure access

Najprije sami registrirajte redakciju unosom osnovnih službenih podataka. Sustav će odmah izraditi jedinstvenu šifru poziva i osobni pristupni kod. Spremite obje šifre, njima otvorite puni obrazac te se kasnije ponovno prijavite i nastavite popunjavanje spremljenog nacrta.

First, register your newsroom by entering its basic official details. The system will immediately create a unique invitation reference and personal access code. Save both credentials, use them to open the full application, and use the same credentials later to sign in again and continue your saved draft.
