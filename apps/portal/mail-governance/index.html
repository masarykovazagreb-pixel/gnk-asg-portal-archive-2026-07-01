Mail Identity & Routing Governance

Review-only contract for sender identity, group-platform context and the ten-centre operational routing model used by GNK ASG Mail Studio, automatic acknowledgements, Media operations and mail evidence records.

REVIEW ONLY · NO PRODUCTION ACTIVATION

Identity hierarchy

Platform context: GNK DINAMO Ltd. Group Enterprise Digital Platform
Regional operating company: GNK ASG d.o.o.
Message identity: the selected Office, Legal, Media, Press, IT, Assistant, Director, Executive or UBO sender profile
A message sent from a GNK ASG d.o.o. mailbox must identify the actual sender profile and regional operating company. The GNK DINAMO Ltd. Group reference provides platform context and must not obscure which entity sent or answered the message.

Directional centre labels

Outgoing: Sending Global Service Centre

Incoming acknowledgement: Receiving Global Service Centre

One message receives one selected centre. The same centre must remain consistent in headers, visible signature or acknowledgement, audit result, forwarding metadata and Mail Sync evidence.

Ten-centre rotation

New York, United States
London, United Kingdom
Paris, France
Frankfurt, Germany
Dubai, United Arab Emirates
Singapore, Singapore
Tokyo, Japan
Sydney, Australia
Toronto, Canada
Zurich, Switzerland

The selection is cryptographically random. The immediately preceding centre is excluded from the next selection within the same inbound or outbound routing scope.

Accuracy boundary

The centre label describes the digital operational routing context assigned to a message. It does not, by itself, claim that a registered branch, permanent establishment, staffed physical office or licensed local entity exists in that city.

Legal notices, invoices, contractual documents and regulated communications must continue to identify the responsible legal entity independently of the routing-centre label.
