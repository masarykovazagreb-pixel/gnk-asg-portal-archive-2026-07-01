FRA issues model agreement for brokerage firms, securities borrowers

21 أكتوبر 2026 · المصدر: Daily News Egypt (English) ↗

ملخص النص الأصلي

The Financial Regulatory Authority (FRA), chaired by Islam Azzam, has issued a model agreement governing the relationship between brokerage firms and clients borrowing securities for sale, marking another step towards the anticipated official launch of short selling in Egypt. The move follows the introduction of a new regulatory framework under FRA Board Decision No. 155
