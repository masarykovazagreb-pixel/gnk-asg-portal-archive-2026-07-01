Methodology

How the edition is made: reading public feeds, checking the link, extracting the headline and summary, reviewing how the story is reported, and publishing with full attribution.

  1. We do not republish the full article or rewrite it as our own news.
  2. Every record carries a direct link to the original and its publication date.
  3. Figures and claims from headlines are marked as things to check, not as confirmed facts.
  4. If a source is unavailable, the edition says so instead of filling the gap with invented content.
  5. Corrections are published on the same page, with a date.
