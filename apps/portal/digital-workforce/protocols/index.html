Operating Protocols

The Digital Workforce may classify, research, compare options, draft and execute only within defined authority. Binding external actions remain subject to named human approval.

Review-only: these protocols remain noindex,nofollow,noarchive until controlled approval. No external execution is authorised here.
HR ugovor pregleda: Digital Workforce smije pripremiti istraživanje, nacrt i opcije, ali ne smije sam odobriti javnu, pravnu, financijsku ili produkcijsku radnju.
EN review contract: Digital Workforce may prepare research, drafts and options, but may not self-approve public, legal, financial or production actions.

End-to-end operating flow

01Receive and identify objective
02Classify data, risk and owner
03Research and cite sources
04Propose at least two options
05Peer review and approval route
06Decision by authorised role
07Controlled execution or stop
08Verification and rollback if needed
09Audit log and outcome review
10Escalation and corrective action

Proposal protocol

minimumOptions is enforced before recommendation. A profile may prepare options but cannot self-approve public or binding work.

minimumOptions: 2selfApproval: forbiddenauditRequired: true

Always requires human approval

  • Production deploy or rollback.
  • Infrastructure or access changes.
  • Financial or contractual representation.
  • Registry, legal, public statement or media release.
  • Sender reputation or campaign execution.

Stop and rollback protocol

1Stop execution when route, sender, data source or approval state is uncertain.
2Preserve evidence, affected URL, workflow run, commit SHA and visible browser state.
3Rollback only through review branch or explicit authorised production procedure.

Audit and escalation

AEvery proposal includes source, risk class, owner and approval path.
BEvery visible UI route must disclose review-only state and indexing lock.
CEscalate missing data, failed CI, failed browser evidence or unsafe runtime ambiguity.
