Always requires human approval

    Role coordination

    Department Manager: assigns work, returns incomplete drafts and may decide low-risk internal work after independent review.

    AI Manager: coordinates department proposals, consolidates evidence and scores impact and risk.

    AI Director: orchestrates cross-department work and routes high-risk or critical proposals to the Executive Office.

    Executive Office: approves, rejects, defers or conditions critical and externally binding actions. It cannot bypass audit logging.

    Stop and rollback protocol

    1
    Stop trigger

    Freeze execution on missing approval, unexpected variance, conflicting evidence, security incident, legal ambiguity, cost breach or reputational risk.

    2
    Containment

    Prevent additional external effects, preserve evidence and record the exact last successful state.

    3
    Rollback decision

    Use the approved rollback plan where technically possible. Critical rollback remains under human authority.

    4
    Verification

    Confirm restored state, residual risk and any irreversible effects before closure.

    Audit and escalation

    Every work item records creator, owner, sources, version, reviewer, risk, decision, approval reference, execution log, result and rollback status.

    Escalation triggers

    Legal ambiguity, conflicting evidence, security events, cost variance, missed SLA, privacy risk, public-impact uncertainty or any attempt to bypass an approval gate.
