Corporate Governance System

One group. Separate companies. Connected governance.

Group level

President, Group Supervisory Board, Group Executive Board, Executive Office and Global Operations Center coordinate the group framework.

Company level

Each company may have its own directors, departments, calendars, local meetings, minutes, decisions and operating rules.

Digital operations level

AI-supported managers, workers and workflow agents operate transparently as a Digital Operations Team, not as fictional real employees.

Operator OS

99% automation. 1% presidential decision.

Automatic execution zone

  • SEO and metadata checks
  • Internal categorization and routing
  • Draft preparation
  • Health checks and status reports
  • Low-risk correction proposals

Approval required zone

  • Production deploy
  • Mass email sending
  • DNS, Cloudflare, secrets and credentials
  • Legal, financial, ownership and governance statements
  • External press statements, filings and contracts
Control principle: Operator OS may prepare, analyze, propose, route, draft and record. Binding, sensitive or production-impacting actions require approval by the President.
