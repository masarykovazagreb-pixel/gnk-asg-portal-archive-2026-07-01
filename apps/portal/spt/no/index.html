SHOWCASE · FOTBALL · USA

Coastline United SC: én kampuke med SPT

Et illustrerende scenario for en profesjonell klubb i California: hvordan et støtteapparat på rundt tretti personer jobber fra mandag til kampdag med én kilde til sannhet.

  1. MON

    Recovery

    The morning wellness questionnaire (sleep, fatigue, muscle soreness, mood) and HRV from the watch arrive before breakfast. Every player gets a readiness score of 0–100; those in the red get an individual recovery plan.

  2. TUE–THU

    Training

    The vest measures distance by speed zone, sprints, accelerations and PlayerLoad. The strength & conditioning coach sees live who is below and who is above the target dose for that day of the microcycle.

  3. FRI

    Decision

    The AI model combines ACWR, readiness, stride asymmetry and injury history. Player #6 shows an ACWR of 1.52 and a 38 % load spike — the staff reduces his minutes before an injury happens.

  4. SAT

    Match day

    The edge server at the stadium receives 22 sensors in real time. The bench sees speeds, heart rate, sprints and alerts; the analyst in the control room compares two players on a half-pitch view.

  5. SUN

    Report

    Automatic match and weekly reports: top 5 by load, new flags, recovery recommendations. Print or PDF for the coach, a mobile summary for the player.

PROGRAMVARE I AKSJON

Dashbord, analyser og rapporter

Hva støtteapparatet ser på laptop, storskjerm, nettbrett ved sidelinjen og telefon — og hvorfor hver visning teller.

Live-dashbord

Alle spilleres posisjoner 20 ganger i sekundet, stilling, hastigheter og belastning — for benken og analytikeren, uten forsinkelse.

Profilradar, ukentlig belastning med flagg, ACWR-måler og varmekart: risikoen synes før skaden.

Kontrollrom

Nettbrett ved sidelinjen

Utøverens app

ENHETSØKOSYSTEM

Maskinvare som måler det som teller

Hver enhet bærer SPT-merket og sender data til det samme utøverkortet — uten manuell inntasting.

SPT-vest

En tettsittende kompresjonsvest; sensoren ligger stille mot ryggen, så GPS-spor og akselerasjoner blir rene. Vaskbar, puster, størrelser fra ungdomsklasser.

Hvorfor: A poorly fixed sensor means noise in acceleration data and false sprints.

Lomme mellom skulderbladene

Sensoren sitter mellom skulderbladene — kroppens mest stabile punkt under løp, med best fri sikt mot himmelen for satellittsignalet.

Hvorfor: Sensor placement directly determines the accuracy of speed and load.

SPT Pod-sensor

GNSS 20 Hz (GPS, Galileo, GLONASS, BeiDou), 9-akset IMU opptil 1000 Hz, UWB og BLE i et IP67-kabinett. Bytter selv fra GPS til UWB når spillet flytter innendørs.

Hvorfor: Impacts, landings and changes of direction last milliseconds — 1000 Hz captures them.

SPT-klokke

Puls, HRV, søvn og daglig restitusjon; en vibrasjon varsler når utøveren forlater målsonen. For en mosjonist er klokka alene allerede hele SPT.

Hvorfor: HRV is the most sensitive non-invasive indicator of fatigue and recovery.

Pulsbelte

Puls slag for slag for intervalltrening, vannpolo og roing — der den optiske håndleddssensoren tar feil.

Hvorfor: TRIMP and heart-rate zones require an exact R-R interval.

SPT-vekt

Bioimpedans: vekt, muskler, fett, vann, visceralt fett og basalstoffskifte — rett inn i kortet og måltidsplanleggeren.

Hvorfor: Losing 2 % of body mass to dehydration measurably reduces performance.

UWB-ankre

Ankre i hallens tak gir posisjonen med omtrent ±10 cm nøyaktighet der GPS ikke virker: volleyball, basketball, håndball, futsal, hockey.

Hvorfor: UWB measures the time of flight of radio pulses in nanoseconds and is robust against signal reflections.

SPT Edge One

En stadionserver tar imot alle sensorer, beregner måltallene lokalt og sender dem til benken på under ett sekund — også når internett faller ut.

Hvorfor: A substitution decision must not wait for the cloud.

SCIENCE

Key features — what they do and why they matter

Behind every number in SPT is a method from sports science. These are the most important ones.

ACWR

Load that builds, not breaks

What it does

For every athlete SPT calculates the ratio of acute (7-day) to chronic (28-day) load from GPS metrics and perceived exertion (sRPE × minutes).

Why it matters

Sudden load spikes are associated with a higher risk of soft-tissue injuries. A zone of roughly 0.8–1.3 is considered the 'sweet spot', while values above ~1.5 call for caution (Gabbett, British Journal of Sports Medicine, 2016). SPT colours every player green, amber or red — before training, not after an injury.

AI

AI injury-risk prediction

What it does

The model combines ACWR, daily readiness, wellness, stride asymmetry, injury history and age into a single risk score from 0 to 100 and lists the main contributing factors.

Why it matters

An injury is rarely caused by one number. Combining signals gives an early warning with an explanation — the staff decides, the model assists. SPT is a decision-support tool, not a medical diagnosis.

GPS + UWB

Hybrid positioning: GPS outdoors, UWB indoors

What it does

Outdoors the sensor uses multiple satellite systems at 20 Hz; indoors UWB anchors measure the time of flight of radio pulses and deliver positions with roughly ±10 cm accuracy. AUTO mode picks the source by itself.

Why it matters

Indoor sports make up half of the sporting world, and GPS does not work under a roof. The same metric outdoors and indoors means comparable data across the whole season.

BIOMECH

Asymmetry that signals a problem

What it does

The IMU measures the ground contact time of the left and right foot (GCT) and countermovement jump (CMJ) height over time.

Why it matters

Rising left–right asymmetry and a drop in CMJ often precede overload. SPT raises a flag when asymmetry exceeds 6 % — time for the physiotherapist, not another sprint.

RTP

Return to play in 6 steps

What it does

Every injury gets a protocol: pain control → range of motion → strength → running → sport-specific work → full training. Each step has a criterion and a physiotherapist's sign-off.

Why it matters

Returning too early is a common cause of re-injury. Criteria instead of the calendar — and the whole staff sees the same status.

READY

Readiness 0–100 every morning

What it does

Sleep, HRV, resting heart rate, muscle soreness, stress and mood combine into one number with a traffic light.

Why it matters

The same session for a tired and a rested player is not the same session. Readiness sets the dose of the day.

CYCLE

Training adapted to the cycle

What it does

Female athletes log their cycle phase and SPT gives phase-specific recommendations: volume, intensity, recovery and nutrition.

Why it matters

Hormonal changes affect thermoregulation, recovery and perceived exertion. Individualisation instead of averages.

SPT is a performance-tracking and decision-support system; it does not replace a medical examination or diagnosis.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is SPT only for professional clubs?

No. The same system works for a professional club, an academy and an amateur athlete. An amateur needs only the watch and the app; a club adds vests, UWB anchors and an edge server.

Does SPT work indoors?

Yes. Indoors it uses UWB anchors with roughly ±10 cm accuracy, outdoors GPS at 20 Hz. AUTO mode switches the position source by itself.

Is the AI injury prediction a medical diagnosis?

No. It is an early-warning and decision-support system that shows risk factors. The decision is always made by the professional staff.

Who owns the data?

The club and the athlete. Clubs are stored under codes, access is defined by roles, and data can be exported at any time (CSV, JSON, API).

Can manufacturers use SPT under their own brand?

Yes. SPT is licensed as white-label: your brand, our tools, software and servers. SPT already runs inside other manufacturers' devices.

How is SPT installed?

On Windows with one click (a desktop shortcut), on phones and tablets as a PWA from the browser, and on the web with no installation.

Which languages does SPT support?

The app is available in 10 languages, and this page in 24.
