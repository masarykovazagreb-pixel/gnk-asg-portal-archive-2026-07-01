UWB-ankre

Ankre i hallens loft giver positionen med cirka ±10 cm præcision, hvor GPS ikke virker: volleyball, basketball, håndbold, futsal, hockey.

Hvorfor: UWB measures the time of flight of radio pulses in nanoseconds and is robust against signal reflections.
