  1. MON

    Recovery

    The morning wellness questionnaire (sleep, fatigue, muscle soreness, mood) and HRV from the watch arrive before breakfast. Every player gets a readiness score of 0–100; those in the red get an individual recovery plan.

  2. TUE–THU

    Training

    The vest measures distance by speed zone, sprints, accelerations and PlayerLoad. The strength & conditioning coach sees live who is below and who is above the target dose for that day of the microcycle.

    Decision

    The AI model combines ACWR, readiness, stride asymmetry and injury history. Player #6 shows an ACWR of 1.52 and a 38 % load spike — the staff reduces his minutes before an injury happens.

  4. SAT

    Match day

    The edge server at the stadium receives 22 sensors in real time. The bench sees speeds, heart rate, sprints and alerts; the analyst in the control room compares two players on a half-pitch view.

  5. SUN

    Report

    Automatic match and weekly reports: top 5 by load, new flags, recovery recommendations. Print or PDF for the coach, a mobile summary for the player.

ΟΙΚΟΣΥΣΤΗΜΑ ΣΥΣΚΕΥΩΝ

Υλικό που μετρά ό,τι έχει σημασία

Κάθε συσκευή φέρει το σήμα SPT και στέλνει δεδομένα στην ίδια καρτέλα αθλητή — χωρίς χειροκίνητη αντιγραφή.

Γιλέκο SPT

Εφαρμοστό γιλέκο συμπίεσης· ο αισθητήρας μένει ακίνητος στην πλάτη, έτσι το ίχνος GPS και οι επιταχύνσεις είναι καθαρά. Πλένεται, αναπνέει, μεγέθη από τις μικρές κατηγορίες.

Γιατί: A poorly fixed sensor means noise in acceleration data and false sprints.

Τσέπη ανάμεσα στις ωμοπλάτες

Ο αισθητήρας βρίσκεται ανάμεσα στις ωμοπλάτες — στο πιο σταθερό σημείο του σώματος στο τρέξιμο, με την καλύτερη θέα στον ουρανό για το δορυφορικό σήμα.

Γιατί: Sensor placement directly determines the accuracy of speed and load.

Αισθητήρας SPT Pod

GNSS 20 Hz (GPS, Galileo, GLONASS, BeiDou), IMU 9 αξόνων έως 1000 Hz, UWB και BLE σε περίβλημα IP67. Αλλάζει μόνος του από GPS σε UWB όταν το παιχνίδι περνά σε κλειστό γήπεδο.

Γιατί: Impacts, landings and changes of direction last milliseconds — 1000 Hz captures them.

Ρολόι SPT

Παλμοί, HRV, ύπνος και καθημερινή αποκατάσταση· μια δόνηση ειδοποιεί όταν ο αθλητής βγει από τη ζώνη-στόχο. Για τον ερασιτέχνη, το ρολόι μόνο του είναι ήδη ολόκληρο το SPT.

Γιατί: HRV is the most sensitive non-invasive indicator of fatigue and recovery.

Ζώνη θώρακα

Παλμοί χτύπο προς χτύπο για διαλειμματική προπόνηση, υδατοσφαίριση και κωπηλασία — εκεί όπου ο οπτικός αισθητήρας καρπού κάνει λάθος.

Γιατί: TRIMP and heart-rate zones require an exact R-R interval.

Ζυγαριά SPT

Βιοηλεκτρική εμπέδηση: βάρος, μύες, λίπος, νερό, σπλαχνικό λίπος και βασικός μεταβολισμός — κατευθείαν στην καρτέλα και στον προγραμματιστή γευμάτων.

Γιατί: Losing 2 % of body mass to dehydration measurably reduces performance.

Άγκυρες UWB

Οι άγκυρες στην οροφή του γηπέδου δίνουν θέση με ακρίβεια περίπου ±10 cm εκεί όπου το GPS δεν λειτουργεί: βόλεϊ, μπάσκετ, χάντμπολ, φούτσαλ, χόκεϊ.

Γιατί: UWB measures the time of flight of radio pulses in nanoseconds and is robust against signal reflections.

SPT Edge One

Διακομιστής σταδίου: λαμβάνει όλους τους αισθητήρες, υπολογίζει τοπικά τις μετρήσεις και τις στέλνει στον πάγκο σε λιγότερο από ένα δευτερόλεπτο — ακόμη κι αν πέσει το ίντερνετ.

Γιατί: A substitution decision must not wait for the cloud.

Parameters and analyses

64 parameters SPT measures — and what they show

From a metre of running to a gram of protein: SPT measures movement, mechanical load, heart, sleep, body, nutrition, hydration, environment, tests, wellness and tactics. Every number has a purpose — each one comes with what it shows and why it matters.

Movement and speed

SPT Pod · 20 Hz GNSS / UWB

6
1Total distance m
The volume of a session or match, compared with the target for that day of the microcycle and the positional average.
2Distance by speed zone m · Z1–Z5
The intensity profile: how long versus how fast the session was — the same kilometres can be easy or exhausting.
3High-speed running (HSR) m · > 19.8 km/h
Exposure to high intensity; sudden HSR spikes are associated with hamstring injuries.
4Sprints count · m > 25 km/h
Repeated-sprint ability; fewer sprints in the second half is a clear sign of fatigue.
5Peak speed km/h · % of personal best
If an athlete does not reach about 90% of their maximum for weeks, they lose the “vaccine” against sprint injuries.
6Metres per minute m/min
Relative intensity, comparable across matches and sessions of different length and players with different minutes.

Mechanical load

SPT Pod · 1000 Hz IMU

6
7Accelerations count · > 3 m/s²
Explosive demands on the thigh and calf muscles; GPS alone does not see them precisely enough.
8Decelerations count · < −3 m/s²
Eccentric load — the most mechanically demanding part of play for tendons and joints.
9Changes of direction count · angle
Load on the knees, groin and ankles; key in football, basketball, handball and tennis.
10Accelerometer load AU
The sum of all body movement — it measures load even where GPS cannot see: indoors, in duels, in the gym.
11Impacts and collisions count · g
Contacts, falls and impacts above a set threshold; in contact sports a signal to check on the player.
12Jumps and landings count · cm · g
Jump volume and landing hardness; in volleyball and basketball the main driver of patellar-tendon overload.

Heart and cardiovascular response

chest strap · SPT watch

6
13Live heart rate and % HRmax bpm · %
Internal intensity in real time; the staff sees who is in the red zone while play is on.
14Time in heart-rate zones min · Z1–Z5
Whether the session hit the intended stimulus: aerobic base, threshold or high intensity.
15TRIMP (heart-rate load) AU
How the body experienced the session — the same external work is easy for a rested athlete and hard for a tired one.
16Heart-rate recovery (HRR) bpm in 60 s
How fast heart rate drops after effort; slower recovery warns of fatigue or the onset of illness.
17Resting heart rate bpm
A morning rise of 5 or more beats often precedes illness, overreaching or poor sleep.
18HRV (RMSSD) ms
Autonomic nervous system balance — the most sensitive non-invasive readiness marker; what matters is deviation from the personal norm.

Sleep and recovery

SPT watch

6
19Sleep duration h
Less than 7 hours of sleep is associated with higher injury risk and slower reactions.
20Sleep stages deep · REM · light
Deep sleep drives physical recovery, REM learning and concentration; late matches shift the stages.
21Sleep efficiency and awakenings % · count
Quality, not just quantity: fragmented sleep reduces recovery even after 8 hours in bed.
22Breathing rate in sleep breaths/min
A rise in breathing rate is one of the earliest signs of infection.
23Skin temperature deviation °C
Deviation from the personal norm flags inflammation or illness; for female athletes it also tracks cycle phase.
24Readiness index 0–100
The morning composite (HRV, resting heart rate, sleep, questionnaire): green trains at full intensity, red gets a recovery plan.

Body composition

SPT scale · bioimpedance

6
25Body mass kg
The daily trend; a sudden drop warns of dehydration or insufficient energy intake.
26Body fat percentage %
Composition against the target for the position and season phase; the trend matters, not a single day.
27Muscle mass kg
Muscle loss during injury or a break — the basis of the nutrition and strength plan for the return.
28Total body water %
Hydration and recovery; together with morning mass it gives a more reliable picture than weight alone.
29Visceral fat level
A health marker — especially relevant for recreational athletes and veterans.

The basis for calculating daily energy needs and the nutrition plan.

Nutrition and energy

app · scale · sensors

6
31Energy intake kcal
What the athlete ate — a meal photo, the food database or the nutritionist's plan.
32Energy expenditure kcal
Calculated from heart rate, accelerometer and basal metabolic rate: how much was actually used in training and across the day.
33Energy availability kcal/kg fat-free mass
Below about 30 kcal per kg of fat-free mass the risk of RED-S rises: hormonal disruption, weaker bones, more injuries.

Fuel for intensity; intake is periodised to load — more the day before a match, less on recovery days.

scale · app · weather data

Load and injury risk

SPT analytics · AI

6
43ACWR (acute : chronic) ratio · EWMA
The load of the last 7 days against the 28-day average; about 0.8–1.3 is balanced, above 1.5 risky.
44Monotony and strain index
How similar the days of the week are and how large the total stress is — a uniformly heavy week precedes illness and overtraining.
45sRPE (session RPE) AU
Effort rating × minutes; internal load even without sensors — for youth and recreational athletes.
46Stride asymmetry L/R %
A left–right difference above about 10% warns of compensation, an early injury or incomplete recovery.
47AI injury-risk estimate % · reasons
The model combines load, readiness, asymmetry and injury history and returns a probability, the reason and a recommendation; the decision stays with the staff.
48Return-to-play index (RTP) % of personal norm
After injury it compares speed, accelerations and load with pre-injury values — an objective return criterion.

What the analyses show

Morning readiness

Who can go full intensity today and who needs an adjusted session: a green/amber/red traffic light for the whole squad before the first session.

Session report

Whether the session hit its goal: planned versus delivered load per player and position, with deviations highlighted.

Weekly risk

Who is entering the risk zone: ACWR, monotony, asymmetry and the AI estimate, with reasons and a recommendation for next week.

Nutrition versus load

Whether intake matched expenditure: energy availability, protein and carbohydrate by microcycle day, with a meal and hydration plan.

How a player compares with their own norm, position and league profile; the season trend for scouting and youth development.

When a return is safe: the RTP index compares current values with pre-injury values, step by step.

Thresholds (e.g. HSR > 19.8 km/h, ACWR 0.8–1.3) are reference values from sports science; in SPT they are tuned per club, sport, age and athlete. SPT provides performance and wellbeing indicators, not a medical diagnosis.

SCIENCE

Key features — what they do and why they matter

Behind every number in SPT is a method from sports science. These are the most important ones.

ACWR

Load that builds, not breaks

What it does

For every athlete SPT calculates the ratio of acute (7-day) to chronic (28-day) load from GPS metrics and perceived exertion (sRPE × minutes).

Why it matters

Sudden load spikes are associated with a higher risk of soft-tissue injuries. A zone of roughly 0.8–1.3 is considered the 'sweet spot', while values above ~1.5 call for caution (Gabbett, British Journal of Sports Medicine, 2016). SPT colours every player green, amber or red — before training, not after an injury.

AI

AI injury-risk prediction

What it does

The model combines ACWR, daily readiness, wellness, stride asymmetry, injury history and age into a single risk score from 0 to 100 and lists the main contributing factors.

Why it matters

An injury is rarely caused by one number. Combining signals gives an early warning with an explanation — the staff decides, the model assists. SPT is a decision-support tool, not a medical diagnosis.

GPS + UWB

Hybrid positioning: GPS outdoors, UWB indoors

What it does

Outdoors the sensor uses multiple satellite systems at 20 Hz; indoors UWB anchors measure the time of flight of radio pulses and deliver positions with roughly ±10 cm accuracy. AUTO mode picks the source by itself.

Why it matters

Indoor sports make up half of the sporting world, and GPS does not work under a roof. The same metric outdoors and indoors means comparable data across the whole season.

BIOMECH

Asymmetry that signals a problem

What it does

The IMU measures the ground contact time of the left and right foot (GCT) and countermovement jump (CMJ) height over time.

Why it matters

Rising left–right asymmetry and a drop in CMJ often precede overload. SPT raises a flag when asymmetry exceeds 6 % — time for the physiotherapist, not another sprint.

RTP

Return to play in 6 steps

What it does

Every injury gets a protocol: pain control → range of motion → strength → running → sport-specific work → full training. Each step has a criterion and a physiotherapist's sign-off.

Why it matters

Returning too early is a common cause of re-injury. Criteria instead of the calendar — and the whole staff sees the same status.

READY

Readiness 0–100 every morning

What it does

Sleep, HRV, resting heart rate, muscle soreness, stress and mood combine into one number with a traffic light.

Why it matters

The same session for a tired and a rested player is not the same session. Readiness sets the dose of the day.

CYCLE

Training adapted to the cycle

What it does

Female athletes log their cycle phase and SPT gives phase-specific recommendations: volume, intensity, recovery and nutrition.

Why it matters

Hormonal changes affect thermoregulation, recovery and perceived exertion. Individualisation instead of averages.

SPT is a performance-tracking and decision-support system; it does not replace a medical examination or diagnosis.

Προσωπική καρτέλα, ετοιμότητα κάθε πρωί, στόχοι και πρόοδος. Τα δεδομένα σου, η καριέρα σου.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is SPT only for professional clubs?

No. The same system works for a professional club, an academy and an amateur athlete. An amateur needs only the watch and the app; a club adds vests, UWB anchors and an edge server.

Does SPT work indoors?

Yes. Indoors it uses UWB anchors with roughly ±10 cm accuracy, outdoors GPS at 20 Hz. AUTO mode switches the position source by itself.

Is the AI injury prediction a medical diagnosis?

No. It is an early-warning and decision-support system that shows risk factors. The decision is always made by the professional staff.

Who owns the data?

The club and the athlete. Clubs are stored under codes, access is defined by roles, and data can be exported at any time (CSV, JSON, API).

Can manufacturers use SPT under their own brand?

Yes. SPT is licensed as white-label: your brand, our tools, software and servers. SPT already runs inside other manufacturers' devices.

How is SPT installed?

On Windows with one click (a desktop shortcut), on phones and tablets as a PWA from the browser, and on the web with no installation.

Which languages does SPT support?

The app is available in 10 languages, and this page in 24.
