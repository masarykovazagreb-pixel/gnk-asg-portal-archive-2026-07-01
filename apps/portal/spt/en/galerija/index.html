SPT · Sports Performance Tracking

Gallery — SPT

GPS + UWB sensors, AI injury-risk prediction, a live control room and analytics for 75 sports. Produced by GNK ASG · Nermin Sefić.

Software

  • Live dashboard

    Positions of every player 20 times per second, score, speeds and load — for the bench and the analyst, with no delay.

  • AI analytics

    Profile radar, weekly load with flags, an ACWR gauge and a heatmap: risk becomes visible before the injury.

  • Control room

    Up to 12 live tiles; each shows what you choose — the map, heart rate, speeds, alerts or a two-player comparison.

  • Sideline tablet

    The coach on the touchline has the same data as the analyst: who is dropping in intensity, who is in the red heart-rate zone.

  • Athlete app

    Readiness every morning, sleep, resting heart rate and weekly load — the athlete understands their day.

  • A sprint in real time

    Speed, heart rate, zone and movement trail of the selected player; the top-speed leaderboard updates live.

  • Live risk alert

    When ACWR crosses the threshold, the dashboard raises a red alert with the reason and a substitution recommendation.

  • The match in numbers

    Team distance, high-speed running, sprints and average heart rate — every second recorded for the report.

Devices

  • SPT vest

    A body-fitted compression vest; the sensor stays still against the back, so the GPS trace and accelerations are clean. Washable, breathable, sizes from youth categories upwards.

  • Pocket between the shoulder blades

    The sensor sits between the shoulder blades — the steadiest point of the body while running, with the best view of the sky for satellite signal.

  • SPT Pod sensor

    GNSS 20 Hz (GPS, Galileo, GLONASS, BeiDou), 9-axis IMU up to 1000 Hz, UWB and BLE in an IP67 housing. It switches from GPS to UWB automatically when play moves indoors.

  • SPT watch

    Heart rate, HRV, sleep and daily recovery; a vibration alerts the athlete when they leave the target zone. For amateurs, the watch alone is the whole SPT.

  • Chest strap

    Beat-by-beat heart rate for interval training, water polo and rowing — where an optical wrist sensor gets it wrong.

  • SPT scale

    Bioimpedance: weight, muscle, fat, water, visceral fat and basal metabolic rate — straight into the athlete record and the meal planner.

  • UWB anchors

    Anchors on the arena ceiling deliver position with roughly ±10 cm accuracy where GPS cannot work: volleyball, basketball, handball, futsal, hockey.

  • SPT Edge One

    A stadium server receives every sensor, calculates metrics locally and sends them to the bench in under a second — even when the internet fails.

Showcase

  • Match day in the USA

    Showcase scenario: professional football in California, every player tracked in real time.

  • Training with SPT vests

    Sprint drills with speed, acceleration and load measured for every player.

  • Amateur athletes

    Watch and app: heart rate, sleep and readiness for anyone who trains — from a morning run to a marathon.

  • Indoor sports with UWB

    UWB anchors on the ceiling deliver centimetre-level positions where GPS cannot work.

  • SPT Cloud for partners

    The servers, tools and software that also power other manufacturers' devices.
