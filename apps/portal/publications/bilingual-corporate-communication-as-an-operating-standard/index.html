Bilingual Corporate Communication as an Operating Standard

A bilingual portal should deliver the same meaning, actions and governance context in Croatian and English.

Published 5 July 2026GNK ASG Intelligence DeskResponsible editor: Nermin Sefić

Equal meaning matters more than literal wording

Company names, project status, approval levels and financial periods must remain consistent across languages. A literal translation can still create different expectations.

Paired routes should offer equal functionality

HR and EN pages should link to each other, use correct canonical and hreflang metadata and provide the same key actions.

Operating standard: controlled terminology, paired routes, equal actions and editorial review.

Human review remains necessary

Automated translation can accelerate drafting, but terminology, figures, dates and links still require verification before publication.
