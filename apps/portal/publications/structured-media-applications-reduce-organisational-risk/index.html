Structured Media Applications Reduce Organisational Risk

A single form connects the required information in one controlled workflow instead of a chain of disconnected messages and versions.

Published 5 July 2026GNK ASG Intelligence DeskResponsible editor: Nermin Sefić

One form creates one record

Standardised input reduces duplicates, incomplete entries and manual copying. Each application receives a clear status and follows the same review process.

Statuses make the workflow measurable

Received, under review, update required, approved, declined and closed provide an understandable operating sequence.

Operating standard: keep the public application simple and conduct review and decisions in the protected Media Command Centre.

Public and administrative layers remain separate

The public portal provides the application and general guidance. Internal review and status management remain protected.
