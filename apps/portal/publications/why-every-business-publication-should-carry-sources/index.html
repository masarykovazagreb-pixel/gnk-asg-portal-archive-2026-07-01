Editorial standard

Why Every Business Publication Should Carry Sources

A source, date, link and version make a published statement easier to verify.

Use a traceable reference

Figures, dates and status statements should point to the document or register from which they were taken.

Record the verification date

A date and version help readers distinguish current information from an older record.

Standard: source · date · link · version · visible update.
