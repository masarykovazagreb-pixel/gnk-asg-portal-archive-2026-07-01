Artificial Intelligence with Mandatory Human Review

AI can structure data and prepare a draft, but the final version must contain verified sources, accurate figures and a responsible editorial decision.

Published 5 July 2026GNK ASG Intelligence DeskResponsible editor: Nermin Sefić

AI supports preparation

Artificial intelligence can accelerate summarisation, note organisation, version comparison and draft structure. The result remains a draft until reviewed.

Sources and figures require verification

Dates, company names, roles and measurable claims should be checked against an original document or approved data register.

Editorial standard: verify facts, recency, attribution, links and the distinction between confirmed information, estimates and projections.

The final decision remains human

A responsible person determines whether the content is clear, complete and suitable for publication.
