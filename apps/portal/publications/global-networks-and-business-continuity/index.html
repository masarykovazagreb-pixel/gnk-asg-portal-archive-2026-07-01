Global Networks and Business Continuity

A location network becomes operationally useful only when responsibilities, access, communication and recovery procedures are clearly connected.

Published 5 July 2026GNK ASG Intelligence Desk

Locations need assigned responsibilities

Each operating location should have a defined function, timezone, escalation path and connection to the relevant company and project.

Data access should support continuity

Approved information must remain available during local disruption, while private records stay protected through role-based access.

Continuity standard: owner · location · backup path · communication channel · recovery checkpoint.

Testing matters

A continuity plan becomes credible when its recovery steps, contact paths and backup sources are tested and recorded.
