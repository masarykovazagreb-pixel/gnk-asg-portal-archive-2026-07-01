Media critique — what can be measured

This page does not judge whether a story is true. It measures form: how often headlines use numbers, superlatives, questions and unnamed sources, and how long the summary is. Everything is computed from published reviews and their sources.

Method

Every published review from the eight channels is assigned to the source in its tag. Percentages are shares of that source’s reviews. Sources with at least eight reviews are included. A number in a headline is not a flaw; the question is whether the text explains it. A superlative is not a lie; the question is what supports it.
