Brand Usage Guidelines

Exact legal names

  • GNK ASG d.o.o.
  • GNK DINAMO Ltd.

Do not abbreviate or alter either legal name in legal, press or corporate documents.

Identity protection

Use of a legal company name must not be presented as proof of identity, ownership, affiliation, representation or authorisation by a football club or another third party unless an approved legal statement expressly supports that claim.

Logos and marks

Only an explicitly approved logo package may be supplied for publication. Draft logos, generated concepts and visual previews remain non-final.

Facts and financial information

Use only facts included in the approved factsheet and supported by source evidence. Do not add employee counts, valuations, consolidated figures or group claims without approval.

Contact and corrections

Media requests, fact checks and correction requests must be submitted through the official GNK ASG contact form and routed to Media / Press or Legal.
