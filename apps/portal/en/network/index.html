Why separate sites, not sections

A topic with its own domain has its own sitemap, feed and archive. When one site changes, the others do not suffer — and the reverse holds too. That is the difference between a network and one large site buckling under its own weight.

The link is mutual: every edition carries gnk-asg.hr and this hub in its bar and footer, and the hub links to every edition. The backlink is not begged for — it is structural.

There is one newsroom. The signature is the same, the standard is the same, and a correction is published where the error was made.

What the network is — and is not

  • It isfifteen standalone sites with their own hosting, sitemap, feed and robots.txt — each with the full SEO layer: canonical, hreflang, OG, Twitter card, JSON-LD and hashtags.
  • It isa network of reciprocal links: editions point to the parent site, the parent points to the editions, and the hub is a list anyone can check.
  • It is nota link farm. No site exists only to carry a link; each has content, sources and a method page.
  • It is nota news robot. The five permanent editions do not depend on a daily feed. Only the election desk is live, and only temporarily.
