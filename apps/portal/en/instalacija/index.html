Install the Portal via Chrome

The GNK ASG portal can be installed as an app for fast access to corporate data, the market monitor, business news and the Intelligence Desk.

1

Open in Chrome

On an Android phone or computer, open the GNK ASG portal homepage in the Google Chrome browser.

2

Choose to install

On the homepage, press the Install in Chrome button, or in the Chrome menu choose Install app.

3

Launch the app

After confirming, the GNK ASG icon appears on your home screen or in the list of installed apps.

Android phone

Chrome → open the portal → Install in Chrome. If the option doesn't appear, tap the three dots and choose Install app or Add to Home screen.

Desktop

Chrome or Edge may show an install icon in the address bar. Click it, then choose Install. The portal will open in its own window.

Open the app and install it directly.

The Chrome install button is at the top of the portal homepage.
