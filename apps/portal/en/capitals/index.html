AKTUAL MEDIA · capitals

Capitals — one standard for every covered country

The network follows one rule: a country is covered from its capital, not from whichever city happens to be biggest. Where the biggest city is not the capital — New York, São Paulo, Sydney — it keeps its own edition, and the capital gets one of its own. Nothing here is announced before it exists: a capital without a verified source list stays marked as in preparation.

What every capital gets — the same eight things

  • vlastita adresa /kanal/<grad>/ na gnk-asg.hr, sa sitemapom, feedom i manifestom
  • znak u familiji AKTUAL MEDIA (navy kuća, crveno izdanje) i atlas grafika u paleti izdanja
  • okvir na jeziku zemlje: navigacija, oznake, povratni link i podnožje — ne samo naslovi
  • SEO sloj: canonical, hreflang za jezik zemlje i engleski, x-default, OG i Twitter kartica, JSON-LD, hashtagovi
  • mreža: blok od 20 ulaza i paukova mreža sa svim 32 adrese, obostrano
  • dnevni ciklus iz provjerene liste izvora; svaki zapis nosi link na izvornik i datum
  • metodologija i popis izvora na jeziku izdanja
  • isti testovi: parity, mreža, SEO matrica, audit bez grešaka

How a new capital is added, step by step

  1. 1. Add the country and capital to apps/region/capitals.json (this list).
  2. 2. Add the channel to apps/region/channels.json with a verified source list, palette, time zone and continent.
  3. 3. Add the mark to scripts/build-aktual-media-logos-v1.py and run it; add the atlas artwork and run scripts/build-atlas-art-v1.mjs.
  4. 4. Run the region build, hub and audit — the edition gets its pages, manifest, sitemap, feed and network block.
  5. 5. Run test-editions-parity — the new edition must pass before it is published.
  6. 6. Only then remove the "preparing" status: an edition is never announced before it has sources.

The order matters: sources first, then design, then pages, then tests. A page without sources would be a promise the newsroom cannot keep.
