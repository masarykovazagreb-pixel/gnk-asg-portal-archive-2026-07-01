Cibona continues building the roster: Khalif Battle arrives

Croatian champions continue building for the new season — American guard Khalif Battle arrives in Zagreb.

The champions continue building their roster for the new season. Cibona has brought in a new face for the Wolves' locker room — American guard Khalif Battle.

The 26-year-old Battle stands 196 cm tall and arrives in Zagreb after his first professional season with Italy's Trento, where he averaged 12.9 points, 2.6 rebounds and 1.9 assists in Serie A, with EuroCup experience as well.

Before turning professional, he built a notable NCAA path through Butler, Temple, Arkansas and Gonzaga — in the 2022/23 season he was Temple's leading scorer at 17.9 points per game, and he spent his final college season at Gonzaga, winning the West Coast Conference tournament and reaching the NCAA tournament.

Cibona head coach Ivan Rudež noted that Battle is a young, modern player with strong athleticism and the versatility to cover multiple perimeter positions, who — after a season adapting to European basketball in Italy — should now bring a new dimension to the Wolves' game.

Battle primarily plays shooting guard, with a strong ability to create his own shot and drive to the basket — added width for the perimeter of a roster defending its championship title.
