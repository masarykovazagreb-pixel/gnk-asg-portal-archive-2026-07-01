## BOR USA

gnk-bor-usa.pages.dev · https://gnk-bor-usa.pages.dev/ · 5 pages

- [BOR USA — combat sports in America, without the noise](https://gnk-bor-usa.pages.dev/en/) — Boxing and MMA in the United States are not run by one organisation but by some fifty state commissions. Understand that and you understand why the same bout is judged differently in two states — and why the most…
- [Who sanctions a fight: mapping US commissions](https://gnk-bor-usa.pages.dev/en/regulativa/) — There is no single regulator of combat sports in the US. There are some fifty commissions, one association that publishes the Unified Rules, and federal bodies that only get involved in anti-doping and fraud. That is…
- [Safety in combat sports: the physician, the weight, the suspension](https://gnk-bor-usa.pages.dev/en/sigurnost/) — The biggest risk in combat sports is not a single strike but rapid weight cutting, too short a gap between bouts, and the decision to continue when one should not. All three are regulated — and all three can be checked.
- [How to read a fight card, in order](https://gnk-bor-usa.pages.dev/en/karta/) — A card is not a list of names. It is a series of contracts stating the division, the number of rounds, the ruleset and the weigh-in deadline. Read that and you can judge an event before the first round.
- [How BOR USA is made](https://gnk-bor-usa.pages.dev/en/metodologija/) — Three rules: no figure without a source, no claim about a person without a document, and no announcement that cannot be checked. The rest is editing.

## KOŠARKA 360

gnk-kosarka.pages.dev · https://gnk-kosarka.pages.dev/ · 5 pages

- [KOŠARKA 360 — how to read a season, a league and a number](https://gnk-kosarka.pages.dev/en/) — Basketball is followed as a result but understood as a system: rules, schedule, pace and space. This desk does not carry transfer rumours. It explains why something happened — and shows where it can be checked.
- [How to read an NBA season: schedule, fatigue, playoffs](https://gnk-kosarka.pages.dev/en/sezona/) — A season is not one table. It is 82 games spread over seven months, with travel, breaks and different motives in every part of the calendar.
- [European basketball: different rules, different rhythm](https://gnk-kosarka.pages.dev/en/europa/) — European basketball is not “the NBA with fewer points”. It is played under FIBA rules, on a smaller court, with a shorter game and a different relationship to space.
- [Which basketball numbers actually measure something](https://gnk-kosarka.pages.dev/en/brojke/) — A shooting percentage without attempts means nothing. Points without possessions, the same. A number only makes sense when you know its denominator.
- [How KOŠARKA 360 is made](https://gnk-kosarka.pages.dev/en/metodologija/) — No transfer rumours, no “greatest ever”, and no number without a source and the period it covers.

## SCENA ZNANOSTI

gnk-scena-znanosti.pages.dev · https://gnk-scena-znanosti.pages.dev/ · 5 pages

- [SCENA ZNANOSTI — how science actually happens](https://gnk-scena-znanosti.pages.dev/en/) — Science is not a series of discoveries but a series of checks. This desk follows the process: who reviewed a paper, whether it was published before review, what was corrected afterwards, and why something was retracted.…
- [What a reviewer actually checks — and what they don’t](https://gnk-scena-znanosti.pages.dev/en/recenzija/) — A reviewer does not repeat the experiment. They read the method, check the logic and ask whether the conclusion is larger than the data. That helps, but it is not the same as independent verification.
- [Preprints: speed that requires a thinking reader](https://gnk-scena-znanosti.pages.dev/en/preprinti/) — A preprint is a public paper before review. It is not less valuable — only less verified. The difference is that the reader must carry part of the checking.
- [Reproduction: the most important experiment is someone else’s](https://gnk-scena-znanosti.pages.dev/en/reprodukcija/) — A result nobody can repeat is not a result but an event. Reproduction is therefore the least attractive and most important part of science.
- [How SCENA ZNANOSTI is made](https://gnk-scena-znanosti.pages.dev/en/metodologija/) — One rule: the genre is never hidden. A preprint is a preprint, a reviewed paper is a reviewed paper, and a press release is a press release.

## STEP BUDUĆNOSTI

gnk-step-buducnosti.pages.dev · https://gnk-step-buducnosti.pages.dev/ · 5 pages

- [STEP BUDUĆNOSTI — dance, movement and technology](https://gnk-step-buducnosti.pages.dev/en/) — Dance is the oldest movement technology and the youngest Olympic sport. This desk follows both: how movement is measured, how it is judged, where injuries happen and what the stage demands of a body.
- [Dance as sport: panel, categories and score](https://gnk-step-buducnosti.pages.dev/en/sport/) — In competitive dance the winner is not whoever the crowd loves more, but whoever the panel scores against published criteria. Those criteria are not secret — they are just rarely read.
- [Motion capture: from markers to markerless cameras](https://gnk-step-buducnosti.pages.dev/en/tehnologija/) — The same technology that animates films also analyses dance: it records joint positions, speed and trajectory. The difference is that a dancer needs feedback, not just footage.
- [Dance injuries: load, warm-up, return](https://gnk-step-buducnosti.pages.dev/en/ozljede/) — Dancers are not less exposed than athletes — they just report less. Foot, ankle, hip and lower back are the usual sites, and the usual cause is a sudden jump in load.
- [How STEP BUDUĆNOSTI is made](https://gnk-step-buducnosti.pages.dev/en/metodologija/) — Measurement before opinion: every claim about movement needs a method, a sample and a source.

## ZDRAVLJE 360

gnk-zdravlje.pages.dev · https://gnk-zdravlje.pages.dev/ · 5 pages

- [ZDRAVLJE 360 — prevention, sleep and how to read a study](https://gnk-zdravlje.pages.dev/en/) — Health is sold in pills and promises, while most outcomes are shaped by boring things: movement, sleep, food, vaccination and regular checks. This desk writes about those — without miracles and without diagnoses.
- [Prevention: what has been shown, and what is only sold](https://gnk-zdravlje.pages.dev/en/prevencija/) — Prevention is not the same as testing. Some measures lower risk demonstrably, some only reveal it, and some are sold as prevention with no serious review behind them.
- [Sleep: how much, why, and what breaks it](https://gnk-zdravlje.pages.dev/en/san/) — Sleep is not passive time. It is the part of the day when memory consolidates, hormones align and blood pressure drops. Shortage is not compensated with coffee.
- [How to read a medical study in five minutes](https://gnk-zdravlje.pages.dev/en/studija/) — Most health headlines come from press releases, not from papers. Five questions are enough to tell the difference.
- [How ZDRAVLJE 360 is made](https://gnk-zdravlje.pages.dev/en/metodologija/) — Guideline before opinion, absolute risk before relative, and a physician before any advice.

## IZBORI U SRBIJI

gnk-izbori-srbija.pages.dev · https://gnk-izbori-srbija.pages.dev/ · 6 pages

- [Serbia’s election: 96 items from 13 outlets](https://gnk-izbori-srbija.pages.dev/en/) — This desk follows what media in Serbia, Croatia, Bosnia and Herzegovina and the United States publish about the election — and it does not copy, it counts and links. Every item has an outlet, a city, a date and a link…
- [All items: 96 published](https://gnk-izbori-srbija.pages.dev/en/pregled/) — The complete list, ordered by publication time. No filtering by political option and no omission of uncomfortable items.
- [Who writes and how much: outlets, cities, days](https://gnk-izbori-srbija.pages.dev/en/statistika/) — The table shows one thing only: how many items meet the method’s criterion. It does not show bias, reach or truth.
- [Incidents and tension: 11 items](https://gnk-izbori-srbija.pages.dev/en/sigurnost/) — This is not a list of incidents — it is a list of items that mention an incident. The difference matters, which is why it is in the first sentence.
- [Outlets the desk reads](https://gnk-izbori-srbija.pages.dev/en/izvori/) — The desk does not pick outlets by sympathy. The archive carries whatever the eight city editions ingest, and public feeds are read from a fixed list. Both lists are public and change when the source does.
- [How the desk counts, and where the limits are](https://gnk-izbori-srbija.pages.dev/en/metodologija/) — The method is short and checkable: archive, criterion, count. Everything else is interpretation we do not publish.
