STEP BUDUĆNOSTI gnk-step-buducnosti.pages.dev

Dance, step and movement as technology: sport, injury, motion capture and the stage.

  • ENSTEP BUDUĆNOSTI — dance, movement and technologyDance is the oldest movement technology and the youngest Olympic sport. This desk follows both: how movement is measured, how it is judged, where injuries happen and what the stage demands…
  • ENDance as sport: panel, categories and scoreIn competitive dance the winner is not whoever the crowd loves more, but whoever the panel scores against published criteria. Those criteria are not secret — they are just rarely read.
  • ENMotion capture: from markers to markerless camerasThe same technology that animates films also analyses dance: it records joint positions, speed and trajectory. The difference is that a dancer needs feedback, not just footage.
  • ENDance injuries: load, warm-up, returnDancers are not less exposed than athletes — they just report less. Foot, ankle, hip and lower back are the usual sites, and the usual cause is a sudden jump in load.
  • ENHow STEP BUDUĆNOSTI is madeMeasurement before opinion: every claim about movement needs a method, a sample and a source.

Health without miracles: prevention, sleep, movement, and how to read a medical study.

  • ENZDRAVLJE 360 — prevention, sleep and how to read a studyHealth is sold in pills and promises, while most outcomes are shaped by boring things: movement, sleep, food, vaccination and regular checks. This desk writes about those — without miracles…
  • ENPrevention: what has been shown, and what is only soldPrevention is not the same as testing. Some measures lower risk demonstrably, some only reveal it, and some are sold as prevention with no serious review behind them.

How the network is held under AKTUAL MEDIA

  • Canonical stays with the editionEvery page has a canonical on its own domain. This list is an index, not a copy — so there is no duplicate content.
  • One publisher, clearly namedThe publisher is AKTUAL MEDIA d.o.o.; the editor and director is Nermin Sefić. That appears in JSON-LD, meta tags and the footer of every page.
  • Own sitemap and feed per editionEach edition has /sitemap.xml, /feed.xml and /robots.txt. The parent domain holds the index and the combined feed, not other sites’ sitemaps.
  • A correction where the error was madeIf a text is corrected, the correction is published on the page where the error occurred — the index is then refreshed.

Machine access

The page list, the feed and the sitemaps are public. A crawler should not have to guess the structure.
