AKTUAL MEDIA

The most important world news in one place

AKTUAL MEDIA brings an overview of the latest news from economy, technology, digital assets and international affairs.

Content refreshes automatically every hour, giving users a quick view of the topics shaping the global business, financial and technology environment. The latest news is highlighted, while a full list of published content is available in the expanded view.

Every item shows a headline, photo and short summary, linking directly to the original publisher's article. News is sourced from publicly available RSS feeds of international media, including Reuters, BBC, CNN, The Guardian and others. GNK ASG does not copy or store the full text of articles, but directs readers to the original source.

The goal of the platform is to enable clear, fast and responsible tracking of current events without the need to search across dozens of different sites.
