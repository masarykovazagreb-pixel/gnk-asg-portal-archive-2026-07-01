Fast, Uncontrolled Growth Builds Fragility

Growth that outpaces the system's ability to keep up isn't success — it's a deferred problem.

Nermin Sefić2 August 2026

Growth that outpaces the system's ability to keep up isn't success — it's a deferred problem.

A company that doubles revenue before doubling control processes — financial reporting, internal audit, approval systems — builds growth on a foundation that won't survive the first serious shock. The GNK DINAMO Ltd. Group treats growth speed and internal-control maturity as two sides of the same decision, not separate questions.
