Fast Revenue Growth Does Not Mean a Healthy Business

Revenue growth without growth in margin and cash flow often means the problem is only being postponed, not solved.

Nermin Sefić2 August 2026

Revenue growth without growth in margin and cash flow often means the problem is only being postponed, not solved.

Revenue is the easiest to measure but tells the least about real business health when viewed in isolation from margin, cash flow, and receivables quality. GNK ASG d.o.o. publicly presents financial indicators precisely in that broader context, not through a single growth figure alone.

Two companies can post an identical 40 percent revenue increase and be in entirely different states of health: one achieved it through repeat orders from existing clients at a stable margin, the other through one-off, discounted deals that erode profitability.

Every serious business review must therefore look past a single growth figure toward revenue structure, client concentration risk, and how quickly revenue actually converts into available cash.
