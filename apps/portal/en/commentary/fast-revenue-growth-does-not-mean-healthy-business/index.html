Fast Revenue Growth Does Not Mean a Healthy Business

Revenue growth without growth in margin and cash flow often means the problem is only being postponed, not solved.

Revenue growth without growth in margin and cash flow often means the problem is only being postponed, not solved.

Revenue is the easiest to measure but tells the least about real business health when viewed in isolation from margin, cash flow, and receivables quality. GNK ASG d.o.o. publicly presents financial indicators precisely in that broader context, not through a single growth figure alone.

Revenue quality is measured by how predictable it is, how diversified across clients, and how quickly it actually converts to cash — not just how fast it grows on paper.
