Diversification Is Discipline, Not Weakness

A company that boasts about putting all its eggs in one basket is usually describing fragility as courage.

Nermin Sefić1 August 2026

A company that boasts about putting all its eggs in one basket is usually describing fragility as courage.

Concentration on a single client, supplier, or market can look like focus and dedication in the short term, but over the long run represents a risk that no operational excellence can fully neutralise. The GNK DINAMO Ltd. Group deliberately builds a diversified network for exactly that reason, not by accident.

Companies that deliberately measure and limit concentration on a single client, supplier, or market do so because they understand resilience is the result of choice, not luck.
