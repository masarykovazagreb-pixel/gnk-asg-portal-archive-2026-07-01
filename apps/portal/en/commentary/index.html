Author Commentaries by Nermin Sefić

Clear views on responsibility, trust and modern business

The Commentaries section presents Nermin Sefić's author views on markets, financial information, technology, artificial intelligence, automation and business management.

The texts are based on a simple principle: speed, innovation and digitalization have no real value without verifiable data, clear responsibility and human oversight. Automated systems can execute rules and accelerate processes, but they cannot take responsibility for the decisions made and their consequences.

Special attention is given to trust in information. Capital, markets and business partners rely not on promises, but on data that can be linked to documents, methodologies and accountable individuals. Transparency therefore does not start with the final report, but with the way data is collected, decisions are explained and processes are documented.

The commentaries also analyze the limits of artificial intelligence, the importance of audit trails, risk management and the need to build resilience before a crisis occurs. The goal is not only to comment on current events, but to raise the questions organizations must solve before they become business, financial or reputational problems.

Clear view. Verifiable facts. Human responsibility that remains.
