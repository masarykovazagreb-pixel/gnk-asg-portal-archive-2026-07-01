Cibona continues building the roster: American guard Khalif Battle arrives

Home›Commentary›Cibona continues building the roster

Croatian champions reinforce for the new season — American guard Khalif Battle joins the Wolves, after a season in Italy's Trento and a notable NCAA career.

Nermin Sefić5 August 2026

Cibona continues building its roster to defend the Croatian title — the newest name in the Wolves' locker room is American guard Khalif Battle.

The 26-year-old Battle stands 196 cm tall and arrives in Zagreb after his first professional season with Italy's Trento. In Serie A he averaged 12.9 points, 2.6 rebounds and 1.9 assists, with EuroCup experience as well.

Before turning professional, he built a notable NCAA path through Butler, Temple, Arkansas and Gonzaga. In the 2022/23 season he was Temple's leading scorer at 17.9 points per game, followed by 14.8 points at Arkansas the next year, before spending his final college season at Gonzaga — where he won the West Coast Conference tournament and reached the NCAA tournament, averaging 13.6 points, 3.7 rebounds and 1.5 assists on an excellent 93.2 percent from the free-throw line.

After his college career, he also played in the NBA Summer League for the Phoenix Suns, standing out particularly against the Portland Trail Blazers with a 21-point performance including five assists and four rebounds.

Cibona head coach Ivan Rudež described Battle as a young, modern player with strong athleticism and the versatility to cover multiple perimeter positions — after a season adapting to European basketball in Italy, he is expected to take a further step up and bring a new dimension to the Wolves' game.

Battle primarily plays shooting guard, with a strong ability to create his own shot, drive to the basket, and contribute in transition. His athleticism and energy should add welcome depth to the perimeter of a roster defending the Croatian championship title.
