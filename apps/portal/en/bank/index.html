01

Project and development

The project sponsor says documentation has been submitted to the competent authorities and permissions remain pending. Technical work does not replace regulatory approval; the demo does not access regulator systems.

02

Locally stored scenarios

The demo is a desktop/local application. Most sample data and workflows stay in the browser and local storage on the user's computer. No user account is needed; do not enter personal or banking data.

03

Narrow market-data access

The local server has only two read-only routes for daily public reference rates from the ECB and Central Bank of Kenya (CBK). They accept no instructions or customer data; the app retries them every six hours while it is running.

Network and first run: rate feeds may be unavailable, in which case the demo shows a labelled, dated sample. The optional Twelve Data EUR/USD panel is off unless someone requests and configures it locally; it is market information only, not an executable price or trading service. Windows setup needs internet for local dependencies on first run. The bundle is not a standalone `.exe`; it requires Windows 10/11 x64 and Node.js 22.12+, and TLS certificate verification remains enabled.

What you can actually rehearse

This is an inventory of the current demo build. Data, amounts, bank examples and outcomes are synthetic; a simulator feature is not evidence that a future service is ready or approved.

01Welcome screen, workspaces and data

  • The welcome screen links to separate staff/operations and customer-portal surfaces and offers nine interface languages.
  • More than 1,000 fictional personal and business customers, more than 1,000 sample accounts and several thousand synthetic transaction records.
  • Seven illustrative staff levels: global platform administrator, group banking administrator, country manager, branch manager, supervisor/checker, operations maker and read-only auditor. Role/persona switching is an interface rehearsal—not authentication, tenant isolation or server-enforced access control.

02Accounts, local ledger and payments

  • Transfers between synthetic accounts in the same currency post to a local double-entry ledger.
  • SEPA-shaped outbound instructions wait for a local second-person checker and can be cancelled only while queued. Approval is not external transmission.
  • An incoming-payment rehearsal credits a demo account only after local checker review. A separate interbank workflow changes sample balances among ten fictional institutions in the demo directory.
  • Nothing is sent to SEPA, SWIFT, ACH, a bank, correspondent or settlement network.

03Virtual cards, savings and lending

  • Staff or demo customers can issue standard, premium or prepaid virtual tokens linked to synthetic EUR accounts; adjust online, international and recurring-purchase controls and daily/monthly limits.
  • Prepaid value can be loaded locally within a cap; fictional merchant purchases, token lock/unlock and cancellation are rehearsed in the local ledger. Only a synthetic token suffix is stored—never a PAN, CVV/CVC or PIN.
  • Savings maturity, loan/overdraft application, local checker, simulated disbursement and repayment are illustrative workflows only—no contract, credit decision or real money.

04Cash desks, ATMs and treasury

  • Rehearsals cover teller deposits/withdrawals and local ATM flows.
  • Treasury/vault examples include reserve, nostro, clearing, branch-cash and ATM balances.
  • Figures are not evidence of capital, liquidity, custody, financial statements or a real deposit; everything posts only to the synthetic scenario.

05FX, reference rates and digital assets

  • Multi-currency samples include EUR, currencies in the ECB table, KES and AED; AED is derived from the ECB EUR/USD reference using the stated USD/AED peg assumption. The interface identifies the source, date and any fallback sample.
  • Reference inputs are daily, informational and can become stale—not executable prices. The Twelve Data EUR/USD panel is separate, optional and off by default.
  • BTC, ETH, USDC and fictional GNKC remain offline with static illustrative values. No wallet, keys, blockchain, custody, exchange, trading, public market or asset withdrawal exists.

06Risk, complaints and customer support practice

  • Allowlisted synthetic KYC categories and checklists support personal/business scenarios without identity numbers, document uploads or source checks.
  • AML alerts are illustrative. A local complaint queue supports guarded creation, status history, escalation and a draft response; it sends nothing externally and does not calculate statutory deadlines.
  • The call-centre rehearsal uses synthetic tickets, local notes and staff/customer handling guides. The helper is transparent, local and rule-based—no AI/API model call.
  • There is no identity, sanctions, PEP, adverse-media, beneficial-owner or real-case screening, nor any connected phone, CRM or third-party support service.

07Format checks and documents

  • The local checker demonstrates country-specific IBAN-shaped length, BBAN and MOD-97 checks, plus 12–19 digit Luhn-format checks; the directory uses synthetic bank/BIC-shaped strings and deliberately non-routable identifiers.
  • Entered values are handled transiently in the page, masked in results, and not saved, logged or transmitted. A passing checksum does not prove that an account/card exists or can be reached.
  • Prints, statements, receipts, transaction slips and SEPA/SWIFT-shaped drafts, CSV and JSON carry visible DEMO/synthetic labels; they are not bank confirmations or official documents.

08Bank & Exchange Interop Lab

  • 50 product concepts and 50 runnable local tools: consent/expiry/revocation, statement normalization and data-quality checks, categories/recurrence, liquidity ladders and cash buffers.
  • Tools also cover fee anomalies and cut-offs, batch validation, FX stress and cross-border costs, nostro reconciliation, funds-protection education, disputes/chargebacks, fraud/AML/sanctions/PEP tabletop exercises, maker-checker maps and audit/data-portability exports.
  • Category filters, local activity, sample evidence and a global pause operate on synthetic examples only. No API keys, data exchange, live partner, exchange or bank connection is present.

09Rule-based background simulator

  • In the local desktop app it is enabled by default and, while the process is open, runs one small EUR transfer every five minutes between two low-risk synthetic profiles.
  • It checks the local double-entry ledger remains balanced, records a bounded status event and persists the scenario. It does not call AI, send an external payment, use customer input or make legal decisions.
  • The UI provides pause/resume and 1-, 5-, 15- or 60-minute intervals. You can optionally keep the app in the Windows tray after closing the window and start it in the background at user sign-in; quitting or shutting down stops the simulator.

What can appear in the market · not an Adria Bank offer

A broad catalogue of banking options — for research, not promises.

Banks and financial groups offer different combinations. This is a broad, non-exhaustive catalogue of categories that could be compared during future product design; it is not a list of services Adria Bank offers, plans or has sought regulatory permission to provide.

Market benchmark ≠ available service.Every item below is a general market category. None is a product you can apply for here, nor a confirmed partner, licensing or launch plan. Any future scope would require separate approvals, a country, legal entity, partners and operational readiness.
CATEGORY FOR REVIEW

Accounts and everyday payments

Across different markets, examples include transaction/current and multi-currency accounts; domestic, instant and international transfers; standing orders, direct debits and bill payments.

In the demo: synthetic accounts, a local ledger and payment rehearsals. No real account, payment traffic or external rail.

CATEGORY FOR REVIEW

Cards, digital wallets and travel

Market offerings may include debit, credit and prepaid cards; virtual or physical cards; mobile wallets, tokenization and purchase/travel controls.

In the demo: local virtual/prepaid tokens and control scenarios. No PAN, issuer, processor, network, wallet provisioning or charge.

CATEGORY FOR REVIEW

Savings and term deposits

Providers may offer savings goals, on-demand accounts, fixed-term products and different interest or maturity models.

In the demo: illustrative savings and maturity scenarios. No real deposit, interest quote, contract or deposit guarantee.

CATEGORY FOR REVIEW

Credit and financing

Market examples include consumer, vehicle and mortgage lending, overdrafts, credit lines and business financing—with different rules by provider and jurisdiction.

In the demo: local application, checker and repayment rehearsals. No real underwriting, credit decision, disbursement, contract or debt.

CATEGORY FOR REVIEW

Business and merchant services

Some offerings include business accounts and cards, bulk payments, payroll, invoicing, collections, card/POS acceptance and accounting connections.

In the demo: fictional business personas and simulated flows. No acquiring, live collection, external payroll or business integration.

CATEGORY FOR REVIEW

FX, cross-border and treasury

Market categories include currency exchange, international payments, cash/liquidity management, treasury tools and some forms of trade finance.

In the demo: dated public reference rates and illustrative conversions. No executable FX, correspondent bank, treasury custody or cross-border transfer.

CATEGORY FOR REVIEW

Digital banking and open integrations

Banks may provide web/mobile applications, notifications, budgeting and self-service tools, open banking and secure links to external accounts or apps.

In the demo: local desktop, language picker and personas. No mobile bank, customer authentication, open-banking connection or account sync.

CATEGORY FOR REVIEW

Investing, pensions and protection

Some wider financial groups may have investment, wealth-management, pension or insurance-distribution offerings, often through separate entities and permissions.

In the demo: offline digital-asset examples for education only. No investment, custody, trading, pension, insurance product or partnership.

CATEGORY FOR REVIEW

Support, security and account controls

Market examples include contact centres, self-service, fraud alerts, card locking, authentication, complaint handling and data-protection controls.

In the demo: local call-centre and complaint scenarios. No real support, authentication, KYC/AML screening, fraud monitoring or real-case handling.

Ambition, with clear boundaries

What this project is — and is not.

This page is informational and collects no data. There is no sign-in, account application or financial-services form.

Can I open a real account?

No. Adria Bank is not presented here as an authorised bank, and no real accounts or services are available through this page.

Are the products in the market catalogue approved or planned?

No. These are general categories for market benchmarking, not an adopted offer, application for permission or promise from Adria Bank. No real service is available.

Are the markets shown confirmed?

No. They are research concepts. Countries, sequence and the scope of any future entry depend on required approvals and further review.

Is the December 2027 target guaranteed?

No. It is a conditional project objective for a potential first step outside Croatia, not a promised opening or service start date.

Can I use my own data in the demo?

Please do not. Use fictional data only. The demo does not verify identity and is not connected to real banks, card networks, exchanges or regulators.
