The idea

An architecture, not a single company.

GNK DINAMO Ltd. Group was built as an international business group focused on technology, intellectual property, international business systems and strategic investment — not as a single company, but as an architecture connecting multiple companies, markets and operating centres under one controlled-disclosure model. "From a single room in Colorado — we moved every continent."

Companies

Parent group, operating company, and a network of 45.

GNK DINAMO Ltd. Group (Boulder, Colorado, USA) is responsible for international strategy, capital structure, acquisitions and the development of the strategic portfolio. GNK ASG d.o.o. (Zagreb) is its Croatian development and operating company — specialised software, payment and transaction infrastructure, digital exchange, sports technology. The Group's business model is not based on headcount, but on software, intellectual property and an international operating network of 45 companies and related entities, all active, across five continents — each with its own code. The name, jurisdiction and ownership structure of a confidential company is not a publicly material fact until the transaction is concluded — and in some cases constitutes a protected trade secret of the parties involved. Disclosure follows once that protection ceases to be relevant, not in advance for the sake of announcement.

Nine strategic projects

Deliberate sector diversity.

The Group's new development phase comprises nine strategic projects that deliberately span unrelated sectors — health and sports medicine, sports technology, financial infrastructure, digital assets, education, food industry, industrial production and energy trading — with THE CODE as the ninth, unifying Group-transformation project. Each project carries its own status (from early feasibility review to an existing technology base actively expanding) and its own confidential-company code where applicable. This deliberate sector diversity reflects Group strategy: not relying on a single market or industry, but building a portfolio resilient to any one sector's cycles.

Centres

Ten global centres, stable assignment.

The Group's operational correspondence — from an inquiry received to a reply sent — is handled through ten global centres (Zagreb, Toronto, Mexico City, Bogotá, São Paulo, Dubai, Singapore, Tokyo, Casablanca, Boulder). Each inquiry receives a case number and is consistently routed through the same centre. Part of this processing — personalised replies, operational projections based on market signals — is AI-orchestrated under management oversight and disclosed transparently on the Digital Workforce page.

Mission

To develop and connect technology, financial, sports, health, education, industrial and energy systems into an international business network with high standards of control, documentation and security.

Vision

To build an international group capable of developing and integrating complex projects, technologies and companies across multiple continents, with a clear operating model and measurable results.

Governance model

GNK DINAMO Ltd. Group and GNK ASG d.o.o. operate on a model of controlled disclosure, verifiable data, protection of confidential information, responsible risk management and a clear separation of public, operational and protected systems.

Nermin Sefić is UBO of GNK DINAMO Ltd. Group, Director of GNK ASG d.o.o. and Editor-in-Chief.

Core principles

What a journalist or third party should know about how we publish.

Verifiability

Verifiable data and documented delivery — every public figure has a source (an audited report, an internally reviewed document, or a publicly disclosed location).

Separation

Public content is separated from protected operational functions — Admin, Dashboard, Worker Ops and Mail Studio are never shown publicly or to unauthorised users.

Compliance before activation

Legal and regulatory compliance before market activation — no project is presented as launched before a legal basis exists for it.

Controlled disclosure

The name and ownership structure of a confidential company is not a publicly material fact until the transaction is concluded — and in some cases constitutes a protected trade secret.

Security and audit

Technological security, auditability and access control across all protected systems, with separation of duties and approval required before any production change.

Clear status

A clear distinction between planned, active and completed — a status label accompanies every project and every public claim on this portal.

THE CODE

The end of an acquisition, the start of the next phase.

Everything above leads to a single point. "Nothing will be the same again." On 7 October 2026 at 11:30 ET, in New York, an acquisition phase concludes — and a new one begins. GNK DINAMO Ltd. Group will publicly present three confidential global companies whose identities remain protected until that moment. But that day is not the Group's destination — it is the starting point of the next architecture: the moment the 45-company network formally expands, and the line between "confidential" and "public" moves for the first time since THE CODE began. What comes after 7 October has yet to be written.
