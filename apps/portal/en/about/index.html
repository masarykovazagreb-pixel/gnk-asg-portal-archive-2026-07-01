Our story

From an idea to an international network.

It all began with an idea: that technology, capital, data, projects and companies could be connected into one verifiable system. The idea was built, tested and protected for a long time before it became visible as an international business structure.

1990 · first signal

The beginning was not a company.

The roots of the story reach back to a period of major change in the early 1990s. That is when the founding idea took shape: value is not created by capital alone, but through the connections between people, knowledge, technology and systems.

Development period

The idea had to be protected first.

Before any public expansion, models, code, contractual relationships, documentation, intellectual property and access control were built. What was being created was not published before it had a legal, technical and operational framework.

2023 · formal beginning

The idea gains companies and responsibility.

With the founding of GNK DINAMO Ltd. and GNK ASG d.o.o., a new phase began. International strategy, development, integration, documentation and governance stopped being just a concept and became an organised business system.

Development and integration

Everything enters the same system.

Software, finance, contracts, data, sports technology, documents and projects stopped living separately. They are connected into a shared architecture in which every decision has a version, a status, a responsible function and a trace.

International expansion

The network is not a map. The network is a way of working.

The Group is expanding through 33 connected companies and 12 planned locations. Every point has a code, a function, a responsibility and a link to the shared structure, while full identities and transactions are published in a controlled manner.

Nine projects

One portfolio, several industries.

Healthcare, sport, financial technology, digital assets, education, food, industry, energy and international integration are developed as separate projects within a shared governance model.

1,536 digital functions.

Digital workflow profiles distribute tasks, process data, prepare content, connect projects, track statuses and leave an audit trail. These are not employees, but technical and operational roles within the system.

Acquisitions and the connecting of international companies are not seen as a final goal, but as the opening of a new phase. What was developed and protected for years gains a wider reach, new capacity and a shared direction.

Next phase
