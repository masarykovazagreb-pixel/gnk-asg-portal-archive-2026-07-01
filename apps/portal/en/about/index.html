Corporate profile

A technology, finance and governance group with an international operating model.

GNK DINAMO Ltd. Group is the international Group framework. GNK ASG d.o.o. is its Croatian development and operating company, focused on technology integration, digital solutions, finance disclosure, governance and project execution.

Group

GNK DINAMO Ltd. Group

Strategic coordination, international structure, consolidated reporting and Group-level governance.

Croatia

GNK ASG d.o.o.

Croatian development and operating company responsible for integration, quality assurance, technology delivery and public corporate communication.

Principles

Controlled disclosure

Public information is separated from protected operations. Financial, project and media information is published through dedicated and traceable sections.

Operating model

Integrated execution across technology, finance and governance.

Mission

Build and integrate durable technology and governance systems that support international operations, transparent reporting and long-term value creation.

Public accountability

Maintain clear separation between verified public information, preliminary management statements and protected internal operations.
