Corporate profile

Who we are and how we operate — in full, with no assumptions.

GNK DINAMO Ltd. Group is an international business group focused on developing technology, intellectual property, international business systems, strategic investments and connecting companies across multiple markets. It is the carrier of Group strategy, capital structure, acquisitions, international expansion and strategic portfolio development. GNK ASG d.o.o. is its Croatian development and operating company — its key functions include the development and integration of specialised software, data, payment, transaction and sports-technology systems, project management, documentation, quality control and the coordination of international business functions.

Business model

Why headcount is not the Group's main measure.

The Group's business model is not based solely on the number of employees in one company or one country. It is based on software, intellectual property, international contracts, related companies, expert partners, project administration, regulatory procedures, due diligence processes and an international operating network.

GNK DINAMO Ltd. Group

International parent group responsible for Group strategy, governance, international acquisitions, the strategic portfolio and THE CODE.

GNK ASG d.o.o.

Croatian development and operating company focused on specialised software, information systems, payment and transaction infrastructure, digital exchange, sports technology, data and implementation.

Group network

45 companies and related entities, 33 operating + 12 dormant locations, five continents.

The Group publicly discloses a network of 45 companies and related entities, spread across 33 operating and 12 planned or dormant locations on five continents. The names of individual companies, transactions and the final structure are published only once the relevant processes are concluded and officially activated. Until then, the remaining companies are publicly identified by code, together with a confirmed city and country — with no invented names and no employee counts.

New development phase

Nine strategic projects.

The new development phase comprises nine strategic projects in healthcare, sport, financial technology, digital assets, education, food production, international manufacturing, energy and Group transformation through the THE CODE programme. The Group clearly distinguishes existing operations from future projects: planned projects, feasibility studies, regulatory preparation, due diligence and development activities are not a claim that a given investment is already completed or operational. Every project in the public register carries a clear status label (e.g. planned/feasibility, review/development, pre-launch) precisely to preserve that distinction.

Governance model

GNK DINAMO Ltd. Group and GNK ASG d.o.o. operate on a model of controlled disclosure, verifiable data, protection of confidential information, responsible risk management and a clear separation of public, operational and protected systems.

Core principles

What a journalist or third party should know about how we disclose information.

Verifiability

Verifiable data and documented delivery — every public figure has a source (an audited report, an internally reviewed document, or a publicly disclosed location).

Separation

Public content is separated from protected operational functions — Admin, Dashboard, Worker Ops and Mail Studio are never shown publicly or to unauthenticated users.

Compliance before activation

Legal and regulatory compliance before market activation — no project is presented as launched before a legal basis for it exists.

Controlled disclosure

Controlled disclosure of confidential transactions and companies — names and details are published only once a process concludes, not in advance for the sake of an announcement.

Security and audit

Technological security, auditability and access control across all protected systems, with separation of duties and approval required before every production change.

Clear status

A clear distinction between planned, active and completed — a status label accompanies every project and every public claim on this portal.
