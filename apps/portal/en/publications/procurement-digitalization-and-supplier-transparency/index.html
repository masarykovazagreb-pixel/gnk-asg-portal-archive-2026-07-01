Procurement Digitalization and Supplier Transparency

A digital procurement platform is only as valuable as how much it improves visibility across the whole chain, not just the speed of a single order.

Nermin Sefić2 August 2026

A digital procurement platform is only as valuable as how much it improves visibility across the whole chain, not just the speed of a single order.

Many companies digitalise procurement for speed — automated purchase orders, electronic approval, ERP integration — while the real added value comes only once the platform centralises delivery history, delays, and per-supplier price deviations in one place.

Requiring every new supplier to go through a structured digital evaluation before onboarding, rather than entering data retroactively, builds a database that is comparable and reliable from day one. Without that step, digitalisation only speeds up existing, often incomplete processes.

Requiring structured digital evaluation for every new supplier from the outset builds a comparable, reliable dataset that retroactive data entry never fully achieves.

This structured onboarding process, while requiring more upfront effort, pays dividends through the comparability and reliability of the resulting supplier data.

GNK ASG d.o.o. requires this structured evaluation for every new supplier onboarded across the GNK DINAMO Ltd. Group, without exception.

Organisations that maintain this structured evaluation consistently across their supplier base build a genuinely comparable dataset that supports more sophisticated supplier performance analysis over time. This consistency, established from each supplier's very first interaction with the organisation, proves difficult to replicate retroactively once inconsistent onboarding practices have already taken hold.

GNK ASG d.o.o. applies this consistently across the GNK DINAMO Ltd. Group's supplier base.
