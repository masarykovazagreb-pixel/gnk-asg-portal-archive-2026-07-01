Renewable Energy: Corporate Contracts

A long-term renewable energy purchase agreement stabilises a company's energy cost, but only if the pricing mechanisms are correctly structured.

Nermin Sefić2 August 2026

A long-term renewable energy purchase agreement stabilises a company's energy cost, but only if the pricing mechanisms are correctly structured.

A Power Purchase Agreement (PPA) lets a company lock in a renewable energy price 10 to 15 years in advance, protecting it from wholesale energy market volatility.

The contract structure — fixed price versus one tied to a market index, virtual versus physical PPA — determines the actual level of risk protection, and the differences are often underestimated in initial negotiations.

Companies that view a PPA purely through a sustainability lens, without detailed financial analysis of the pricing mechanism, may take on more market risk than intended.

Transparent reporting on energy origin from PPA contracts is increasingly becoming a condition for participating in public tenders and in large business partners' ESG reporting.

Understanding the specific pricing mechanism within a power purchase agreement, not just its headline terms, determines the actual level of market risk protection it provides.

Detailed financial modelling of pricing mechanisms, conducted independently of sustainability considerations, ensures the contract's actual risk profile is fully understood before signing.

This independent modelling should be repeated periodically throughout the contract term, since market conditions can shift the actual risk profile from what was originally anticipated.

Organisations that build genuine financial modelling expertise around these contracts, rather than relying entirely on sustainability teams unfamiliar with market risk mechanics, negotiate terms that more accurately reflect their actual risk tolerance. This cross-functional collaboration between sustainability and finance teams increasingly determines whether these contracts deliver their intended value.
