Treasury and Counterparty Risk

Counterparty risk calls for exposure diversification, clear limits, and monitoring of collection and collateral quality.

Nermin Sefić2 August 2026

Counterparty risk calls for exposure diversification, clear limits, and monitoring of collection and collateral quality.

Liquidity isn't determined only by the amount of cash but by the reliability of the institutions and partners through which that cash is held or transferred.

A practical framework sets limits by bank, partner, currency, and instrument, and distinguishes legal protection from what's actually available in practice.

Diversifying exposure reduces the likelihood that one counterparty's problem grows into a problem for the whole system.

Regular reassessment of counterparty exposure, not just at the point of establishing a banking relationship, catches deterioration in counterparty stability before it becomes a genuine concern.

Diversifying banking relationships, rather than concentrating with a single counterparty, reduces exposure to any individual institution's potential distress.

Regular monitoring of counterparty financial health, not just credit ratings alone, provides earlier warning of emerging concerns than relying solely on external assessments.

Organisations that combine diversified banking relationships with genuine ongoing monitoring of each counterparty's financial condition achieve considerably more robust protection than either practice alone would provide. This combined approach requires sustained attention from treasury teams, particularly during periods of broader financial market stress when counterparty risk tends to increase most significantly.

This combined discipline matters most during periods of broader financial market stress.

Diversification and active monitoring together provide protection that neither achieves reliably alone.

Sustained attention to this risk category matters most exactly when markets grow least predictable.

This vigilance, sustained through calm periods, is what makes it effective during turbulent ones.
