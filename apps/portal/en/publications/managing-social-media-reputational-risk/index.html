Managing Social Media Reputational Risk

A reputational crisis on social media rarely surprises companies with a predefined response protocol; it only surprises those without one.

Nermin Sefić2 August 2026

A reputational crisis on social media rarely surprises companies with a predefined response protocol; it only surprises those without one.

The speed at which information spreads on social media means the first hour after an incident often determines the total scope of reputational damage, regardless of whether the claim is founded.

Companies without a predefined protocol — who approves the response, what tone to use, when legal gets involved — react slowly and inconsistently, worsening perception further.

Real-time monitoring of brand mentions, combined with clearly defined escalation thresholds, enables early reaction before a topic reaches a wider audience.

Transparent, fast, and consistent communication during a crisis builds long-term trust even when the initial incident was a genuine company failing.

Pre-approved response templates for common scenario types allow faster initial reaction while still preserving room for situation-specific judgement where genuinely needed.

Regular simulation exercises, testing the response protocol against realistic scenarios, reveal gaps before an actual crisis exposes them under far less forgiving conditions.

GNK ASG d.o.o. maintains and regularly tests this response protocol across the GNK DINAMO Ltd. Group, treating readiness as an ongoing discipline.

Organisations that invest in genuine monitoring capability, not just reactive response protocols, gain valuable early warning that allows proportionate intervention before an issue reaches wider audiences. This combination of proactive monitoring and prepared response protocol represents considerably more effective risk management than either capability alone.

Together, these capabilities offer considerably more protection than either alone.

GNK ASG d.o.o. maintains and regularly tests this combined capability across the GNK DINAMO Ltd. Group.
