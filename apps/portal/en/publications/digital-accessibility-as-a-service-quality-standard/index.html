Digital Accessibility as a Service Quality Standard

Accessibility of digital services improves usability, reduces errors, and expands the range of users who can complete a task independently.

Nermin Sefić2 August 2026

Accessibility of digital services improves usability, reduces errors, and expands the range of users who can complete a task independently.

Digital accessibility isn't just a technical requirement but an indicator of service quality.

Clear structure, keyboard navigation, descriptive text, and sufficient contrast benefit all users.

Accessibility measurement should be built into regular quality control, not left for the final phase of a project.

Treating accessibility as a core quality standard, comparable to performance or security, rather than a compliance afterthought, tends to produce better outcomes for all users, not only those with specific accessibility needs.

Regular accessibility audits, conducted by people who actually rely on assistive technology, surface issues that automated testing tools alone consistently miss.

This standard extends naturally across an organisation's full digital presence, from the primary website to every customer-facing application and document format offered.

Involving accessibility considerations from the earliest design stage, rather than retrofitting compliance later, consistently costs less and produces better outcomes than remediation after launch.

Customer feedback channels specifically inviting accessibility concerns, monitored as closely as other service quality metrics, surface issues that formal audits on a fixed schedule can miss between review cycles.

Extending accessibility review to internal tools, not only customer-facing products, ensures employees with accessibility needs receive equal consideration.

This continuous attention distinguishes organisations for whom accessibility is genuinely embedded from those treating it as a periodic compliance exercise revisited only when prompted.

Organisations that embed accessibility metrics into standard product scorecards, reviewed at the same cadence as performance or security metrics, ensure the topic receives consistent attention rather than periodic, isolated focus. This integration also makes accessibility considerations visible earlier in the development process, when addressing them costs considerably less than retrofitting compliance after launch.
