Cybersecurity in the Supply Chain

An organisation's security depends also on suppliers, integrations, and external services that have access to its data and processes.

Nermin Sefić2 August 2026

An organisation's security depends also on suppliers, integrations, and external services that have access to its data and processes.

An attack on a supplier can have the same effect as a direct attack on the organisation.

Assessment must cover access, data exchange, incident-reporting obligations, and the ability to quickly terminate an integration.

Contract provisions only have value once linked to technical controls and verifiable evidence.

A supplier's security failure becomes an organisation's own operational and reputational problem the moment that supplier handles data or systems the organisation depends on.

Contractual security requirements carry limited value without a verification mechanism, since suppliers under commercial pressure to win business have every incentive to overstate their actual practices.

Requiring suppliers to disclose their own subcontractor relationships extends visibility further down the chain than most organisations currently maintain.

GNK ASG d.o.o. applies consistent supplier security standards across the GNK DINAMO Ltd. Group, regardless of a supplier's size or geographic location.

This extended visibility, though harder to obtain, often reveals the actual point of vulnerability more accurately than assessing only the primary vendor relationship.

Suppliers unwilling to provide this visibility may themselves be signalling weaker security maturity worth factoring into the broader risk assessment.

Building these expectations into contract renewal cycles, not only new supplier onboarding, ensures existing relationships are held to the same evolving standard as new ones.

Regular penetration testing that includes supplier-connected systems, not just internal infrastructure, reveals vulnerabilities that isolated internal testing alone would miss entirely.

GNK ASG d.o.o. applies these standards uniformly across its supplier relationships within the GNK DINAMO Ltd. Group, regardless of individual supplier size.
