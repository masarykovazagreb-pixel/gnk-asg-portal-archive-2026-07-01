Due Diligence in the Digital Age

Digital transformation expands the scope of due diligence beyond traditional financial indicators.

Digital transformation expands the scope of due diligence beyond traditional financial indicators.

The traditional due diligence process focuses primarily on financial indicators. In a digital environment, within the informational overview of GNK ASG d.o.o. and GNK DINAMO Ltd., that scope must also include technology, data, and security assessment.

A practical approach adds technology assessment as an equal layer of analysis alongside financial and legal review. Technical debt is often not visible in standard financial statements.

The quality and ownership of data assets are becoming just as important as traditional tangible assets in valuation.

A particular risk is underestimating the cost of integrating technology systems. That risk is reduced by involving the technical team early in the assessment.

An expanded due diligence process reduces the likelihood of subsequent surprises after a transaction closes. This publication is part of the GNK ASG Intelligence Desk system and is informational in nature.

Traditional due diligence checklists increasingly miss risks specific to digital operations, from data governance practices to technology debt that may not appear in standard financial review.

Including technical due diligence alongside financial and legal review, conducted by people who genuinely understand the target's technology stack, surfaces risks a purely financial review would overlook entirely.

This expanded scope has become particularly important in acquisitions involving companies whose core value depends substantially on proprietary technology or data assets.

Building this expanded due diligence scope into standard acquisition procedures, rather than treating it as optional depending on deal size, protects against underestimating technology risk in smaller transactions.
