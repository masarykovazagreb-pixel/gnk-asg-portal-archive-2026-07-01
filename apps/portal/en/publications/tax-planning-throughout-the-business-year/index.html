Tax Planning Throughout the Business Year

A tax liability calculated only at year-end leaves too little room for legal optimisation measures that need time to implement.

Nermin Sefić1 August 2026

A tax liability calculated only at year-end leaves too little room for legal optimisation measures that need time to implement.

Many legal tax-optimisation measures — investment timing, timing of revenue and expense recognition — need to be implemented before year-end, not after.

A quarterly tax-liability projection, rather than only an annual calculation, gives enough time to identify optimisation opportunities while they're still actionable.

Companies that treat their tax liability as a result rather than an actively planned variable regularly miss legal savings available through timely planning.

Collaboration between the finance function and tax advisor throughout the year, not just during filing season, builds a discipline that pays off long-term.

Continuous tax planning, integrated into regular financial decision-making, produces better outcomes than a concentrated effort undertaken only as filing deadlines approach.

This continuous approach also reduces the risk of overlooking planning opportunities that only become apparent when reviewed alongside real-time business developments.

GNK ASG d.o.o. integrates continuous tax planning into its standard financial management practice across the GNK DINAMO Ltd. Group.

Organisations that integrate tax considerations into regular business decision-making, rather than treating tax as a separate, periodic function, identify planning opportunities that a purely compliance-focused approach would miss entirely. This integrated approach requires genuine collaboration between tax specialists and broader business teams throughout the year, not just during dedicated planning periods.
