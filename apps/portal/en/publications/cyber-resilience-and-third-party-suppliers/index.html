Cyber Resilience and Third-Party Suppliers

The weakest point in a system is often an external service whose capacity, security, and recovery plan haven't been verified.

Nermin Sefić2 August 2026

The weakest point in a system is often an external service whose capacity, security, and recovery plan haven't been verified.

A company can secure its own network well and still lose a key function because of an incident at a supplier, platform, or data centre.

Third-party assessment must link security controls to business consequence: which process stops, how long it can stay down, and whether an alternative way of working exists.

The contract must include incident notification, evidence availability, recovery deadlines, data ownership, and an executable exit plan.

A backup supplier isn't a real backup until it's technically tested and contractually activated.

This publication is an original analysis; the sources cited serve as reference documentation.

Extending security requirements to a supplier's own subcontractors, not just the direct contractual counterparty, closes a gap that attackers increasingly exploit as a path into otherwise well-defended organisations.

Regular reassessment of supplier security posture, not just a one-time evaluation at the start of the relationship, accounts for how that posture can deteriorate over time without any change to the contractual relationship itself.

Building this verification into the vendor selection process, not just ongoing management, prevents high-risk suppliers from entering the relationship in the first place.

For critical suppliers, requesting evidence of independent security certification provides more assurance than self-reported questionnaires alone.

This verification process should scale with supplier criticality, applying more rigorous checks to vendors handling sensitive data than to those with minimal system access.

Building this scaled approach into procurement policy from the outset avoids applying inconsistent standards across an organisation's supplier base.
