Managing Software Licenses: Cost and Risk

A mismatch between licenses in use and the actual contract is most often discovered during a software vendor audit, at the most expensive possible moment.

Nermin Sefić2 August 2026

A mismatch between licenses in use and the actual contract is most often discovered during a software vendor audit, at the most expensive possible moment.

Growth in headcount and teams naturally increases the number of software instances in use, but contracted licenses rarely adjust automatically to that growth without active monitoring.

Software vendors conduct periodic licensing compliance audits, and any discovered shortfall is billed retroactively, often with a penalty significantly exceeding the regular price of additional licenses.

A centralised view of all active software contracts, regularly compared against the number of actual users, prevents mismatches from accumulating over time.

Cancelling unused licenses is just as important as making up shortfalls — errors in both directions directly affect cost and legal exposure.

Automated license tracking tools reduce the manual burden of reconciliation, though periodic human review remains necessary to catch discrepancies automated systems may miss.

Building license review into the standard procurement process, rather than treating it as a separate compliance task, catches issues before contracts are signed.

This proactive integration prevents the more disruptive alternative of discovering license shortfalls only when a vendor conducts its own compliance audit.

This integrated procurement discipline also strengthens negotiating position with software vendors, since organisations with accurate usage data can make more informed decisions about actual licensing needs rather than accepting vendor-proposed terms at face value. Regular reconciliation between contracted and actual usage remains essential even after initial procurement discipline is established.

This proactive integration prevents costlier discoveries during a vendor's own compliance audit.
