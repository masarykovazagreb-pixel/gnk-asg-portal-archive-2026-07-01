Managing Model Risk in Business Decisions

Models assist decision-making, but require limits, independent review, and a clear process for when results diverge from reality.

Nermin Sefić2 August 2026

Models assist decision-making, but require limits, independent review, and a clear process for when results diverge from reality.

Every model simplifies reality and therefore carries the risk of a wrong assumption or wrong application.

Model control includes documented inputs, versions, warning thresholds, and independent validation.

A model's output should be an input to a decision, not a substitute for responsible human judgment.

Independent validation of models used for significant business decisions, separate from the team that built them, catches errors that the original developers are least likely to notice themselves.

This independent validation should scale with decision significance, applying the most rigorous scrutiny to models informing the organisation's highest-stakes choices.

This validation process should be documented thoroughly, creating a record useful both for internal governance and for any external regulatory review.

This validation discipline becomes increasingly important as organisations rely more heavily on models for decisions with genuine financial or operational consequences, rather than purely exploratory analysis. Building independent validation into standard model deployment processes, not treating it as optional, reflects the growing stakes involved.

This discipline matters most for decisions carrying genuine financial or operational consequence.

Documented validation creates a record valuable for both internal governance and external regulatory review.

Independent scrutiny remains the most reliable safeguard against errors the original builders overlook.

This validation practice, once established, becomes a standard part of responsible decision-making across the organisation.

This scrutiny, applied consistently, becomes a genuine safeguard rather than a formality.

This final step of independent review closes the loop on genuinely trustworthy model governance.
