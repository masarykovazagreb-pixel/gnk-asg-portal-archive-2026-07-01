Whistleblowing Policy and Internal Control

A whistleblowing channel works only if employees believe reporting won't have consequences for their position.

Nermin Sefić2 August 2026

A whistleblowing channel works only if employees believe reporting won't have consequences for their position.

The existence of a formal whistleblowing channel doesn't guarantee it will actually be used. Employees weigh the risk of reporting against the perceived benefit, and that assessment depends on trust in the anonymity of the process and in whether a supervisor will learn the reporter's identity before the investigation concludes.

Independent operation of the channel outside the direct management line, with a clearly defined deadline for feedback to the reporter, measurably increases both the number and quality of reports. The absence of any feedback after a report is filed is the fastest way for the channel to stop functioning, regardless of formal policy.

A whistleblowing channel that employees genuinely trust, protected by credible anti-retaliation enforcement, surfaces internal control failures considerably earlier than relying on external discovery.

GNK ASG d.o.o. maintains this channel with documented anti-retaliation commitments across the GNK DINAMO Ltd. Group, reviewed periodically for genuine effectiveness.

This periodic effectiveness review should include genuine feedback from those who have used the channel, not only aggregate statistics on cases handled.

Organisations that combine genuine anti-retaliation enforcement with regular, transparent communication about the channel's use and outcomes build the trust necessary for employees to actually use it when concerns arise. This trust, once established through consistent practice, functions as an early warning system considerably more effective than internal controls relying solely on formal audit processes.
