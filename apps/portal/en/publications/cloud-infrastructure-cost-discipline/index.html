Cloud Infrastructure: Cost Discipline

Growth in cloud infrastructure requires the same cost discipline as any other capital expenditure.

Nermin Sefić2 August 2026

Growth in cloud infrastructure requires the same cost discipline as any other capital expenditure.

The elasticity of cloud infrastructure brings operational advantage, but also the risk of uncontrolled cost growth. Within the technology framework of GNK ASG d.o.o. and GNK DINAMO Ltd., that requires the same level of discipline as any other capital expenditure.

A practical approach links every infrastructure cost to the specific business process that justifies it. A cost without a clear process owner is hard to control.

The distinction between fixed and variable infrastructure costs must be visible in reporting, since it affects how future periods are planned.

The most common risk is the gradual accumulation of unused resources. Regular utilisation review directly reduces that risk.

Cost discipline in cloud infrastructure keeps technology growth aligned with business justification. This publication is part of the GNK ASG Intelligence Desk system and is informational in nature.

Regularly auditing actual resource utilisation against provisioned capacity often reveals a gap between what an organisation pays for and what it genuinely needs, a gap that tends to widen quietly over time without active management.

Assigning ownership of cloud spend to the engineering teams actually provisioning resources, rather than leaving it solely with finance, tends to produce more sustainable cost discipline than top-down budget caps alone.

This shared ownership model tends to surface waste faster than a purely centralised approach ever could.

Regular, scheduled reviews of this kind prevent the gradual cost creep that otherwise accumulates unnoticed across dozens of individually small, seemingly reasonable provisioning decisions.
