Geopolitical Risk and Supplier Diversification

Supplier diversification reduces exposure to geopolitical risk, but requires a clear cost-benefit analysis.

Nermin Sefić2 August 2026

Supplier diversification reduces exposure to geopolitical risk, but requires a clear cost-benefit analysis.

Geopolitical risk increasingly affects supply chain continuity. For the informational overview of GNK ASG d.o.o. and GNK DINAMO Ltd. business, supplier diversification represents one of the core tools for reducing that exposure.

A practical approach requires mapping jurisdictional concentration for every key procurement category, not just individual suppliers.

Diversification carries additional coordination cost. That cost must be explicitly weighed against the value of reduced disruption risk.

A particular risk is hidden concentration through subcontractors not directly visible in the contractual relationship. That risk is reduced by requiring transparency across the entire chain.

Balanced supplier diversification turns geopolitical risk into a manageable operational variable. This publication is part of the GNK ASG Intelligence Desk system and is informational in nature.

Concentrating critical supply relationships within a single geopolitically sensitive region creates exposure that can materialise with little advance warning.

Diversifying supplier geography, even at some cost to efficiency, provides genuine resilience against disruptions that purely commercial supplier selection criteria would not anticipate.

This diversification strategy requires ongoing monitoring of the geopolitical landscape, not a one-time assessment made when supplier relationships were first established.

Building diversification requirements into procurement policy from the outset, rather than treating it as a reactive measure after a disruption, produces more resilient supply chains at lower overall cost.

Mapping second- and third-tier suppliers, not just direct contractual counterparties, reveals concentration risk that a surface-level supplier audit would overlook entirely.
