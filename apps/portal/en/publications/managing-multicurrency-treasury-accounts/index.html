Managing Multicurrency Treasury Accounts

A group operating in multiple currencies without a centralised treasury loses visibility into the real liquidity position of the whole structure.

Nermin Sefić1 August 2026

A group operating in multiple currencies without a centralised treasury loses visibility into the real liquidity position of the whole structure.

When each related company within a group independently manages its own bank accounts and currency exposure, the group as a whole loses the ability to see its total liquidity position in real time.

A centralised treasury function, even while individual companies retain operational autonomy, enables netting intra-group receivables and payables before accessing external financing.

Multicurrency cash pooling reduces the need to hold separate liquidity reserves in each currency, freeing up capital that would otherwise be scattered across individual accounts.

A clear policy on who within the group approves intra-group transfers and at what exchange rate, documented and consistently applied, prevents later disputes over intra-group settlements.

Centralised visibility across all currency accounts, even when operational management remains decentralised, gives treasury the full picture needed for effective currency risk management.

Consolidated reporting across currencies, updated in real time where feasible, gives treasury the responsiveness needed to act on emerging currency risk promptly.

This centralised visibility becomes particularly valuable during periods of currency volatility, when delayed awareness of exposure can prove costly.

This consolidated visibility also supports more sophisticated hedging strategies, since treasury can identify natural offsetting exposures across currencies that would remain hidden within siloed, currency-specific reporting. Organisations with genuinely centralised visibility typically achieve more capital-efficient currency risk management than those managing each currency exposure independently.

This visibility proves most valuable precisely during periods of currency volatility.
