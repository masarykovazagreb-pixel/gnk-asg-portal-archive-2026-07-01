Operational Resilience and Business Continuity

Business continuity requires clear priorities, replacement processes, and regular testing of decisions before an actual disruption.

Nermin Sefić2 August 2026

Business continuity requires clear priorities, replacement processes, and regular testing of decisions before an actual disruption.

Operational resilience doesn't begin with a crisis plan but with understanding the processes without which an organisation can't deliver core value.

A practical framework links critical processes, responsible people, backup suppliers, and acceptable recovery time.

Regular scenario testing reveals assumptions that most often become failure points in an actual disruption.

Regular testing against realistic failure scenarios, not just documented procedures, reveals whether an organisation's resilience is genuine or merely theoretical.

GNK ASG d.o.o. tests resilience assumptions against realistic failure scenarios across the GNK DINAMO Ltd. Group on a recurring basis, not as a one-time exercise.

This recurring testing discipline, embedded into standard operational rhythm rather than treated as exceptional, is what ultimately distinguishes genuine resilience from documented aspiration.

Organisations that build this testing discipline into standard operating rhythm, rather than treating it as an occasional compliance exercise, develop genuine organisational muscle memory that proves invaluable when an actual disruption occurs. This distinction between tested and merely documented resilience often only becomes apparent during the moment resilience is genuinely required.

This distinction between tested and merely documented resilience matters most when genuinely needed.

Genuine muscle memory, built through repeated testing, is what separates real resilience from paper plans.

This ongoing testing rhythm, sustained over years, is what genuine resilience actually requires.

Repeated, honest testing remains the only reliable way to know whether resilience is real.

Nothing substitutes for this repeated, honest verification over time.
