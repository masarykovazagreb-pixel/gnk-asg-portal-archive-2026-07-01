Cryptocurrency Payment Resilience: Operational Risk

A company accepting cryptocurrency payments without a clear volatility-management policy takes on market risk that isn't part of its core business.

Nermin Sefić2 August 2026

A company accepting cryptocurrency payments without a clear volatility-management policy takes on market risk that isn't part of its core business.

Accepting cryptocurrency payments expands the potential customer base, but exposes a company to price volatility unrelated to its core business, unless that exposure is actively managed.

Immediately converting received cryptocurrency to fiat currency through a payment processor removes market risk, while holding cryptocurrency as part of the balance sheet consciously takes on that exposure.

A clear, documented, and consistently applied policy on whether received cryptocurrency is held or immediately converted prevents ad hoc decisions that are hard to explain to auditors later.

The regulatory treatment of cryptocurrency income varies significantly between jurisdictions, making tax and accounting preparation just as important as the decision to accept that payment method at all.

Maintaining a clear, pre-defined policy on conversion timing, rather than making ad hoc decisions under market pressure, prevents inconsistent treatment of otherwise similar cryptocurrency transactions received by the organisation.

GNK ASG d.o.o. publishes the market conditions informing its own conversion timing decisions through the Digital Exchange Monitor, offering transparency into an approach that many organisations handle without any external visibility at all.

This policy should be reviewed periodically as market conditions and regulatory treatment of digital assets continue to evolve across different jurisdictions.

Clear internal communication about the reasoning behind conversion decisions helps finance and operational teams understand why timing may vary across similar transactions.

Clear escalation procedures for unusually large or unusual transactions add a further layer of protection beyond standard conversion policy alone.
