ASG Entity Intelligence

A controlled tool for connecting business identifiers, public registry records and digital traces through a cascading enrichment chain. Every relationship must carry source evidence, verification time, match reason and confidence.

Run a controlled search

The public view does not provide open reverse-phone lookup for private individuals. Phone enrichment remains restricted to legitimate business/public contacts and authorized internal workflows.

1. Query Router ready

Detects the identifier type and selects only sources that support it.

2. Source Adapters

Official API, permitted dataset or an internally synchronized database. No assumed direct SQL access to third-party registries.

3. Entity Resolution

Candidates are merged only when enough signals exist. A name match by itself is not proof of identity.

4. Graph Expansion

New verified identifiers may trigger additional adapters subject to maxDepth, timeout and node limits.

5. Evidence Response

The response carries source, timestamp, match reason, confidence and verification status.
