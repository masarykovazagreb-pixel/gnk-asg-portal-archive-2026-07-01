About this edition

This journal presents five food categories — Beef (Classic Beef Stew), Chicken (Simple Roast Chicken), Dessert, Seafood (Grilled Salmon with Lemon Butter) and Vegetarian (Classic Margherita Pizza) — each with an original category description and a classic recipe. Alongside these, the featured recipe "Spotted Dick", a British dessert, includes full ingredients and preparation steps.

Category descriptions and the featured recipe are sourced from TheMealDB, a free, public recipe database. The remaining five recipes are classic, widely known dishes, retold in original wording for this edition.
