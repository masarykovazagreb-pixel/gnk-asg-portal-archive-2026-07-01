AI newsroom without invention

2027-04-01 → 2027-06-30

The agent may reason only from what exists in the repository: headline, source, date and link. Every claim must have a path back to a record.

  • A daily brief assembled from real records, each with its link

Distribution and resilience

2027-07-01 → 2027-09-30

Everything published must be findable: sitemaps, IndexNow and feeds without a hand-kept list. When a source fails, the page falls back to the archive, not to emptiness.

  • Sitemap and IndexNow from the same source of truth
  • A failed source does not change the published count
  • A monthly availability statement
