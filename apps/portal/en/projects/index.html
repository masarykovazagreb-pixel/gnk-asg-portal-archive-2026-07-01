Role of GNK ASG d.o.o.

Development integrator and IP assembler of the Group

GNK ASG d.o.o. integrates partial code, modules, libraries, connectors and other development inputs into deployable software solutions, while providing QA, documentation, configuration and handover support.

GNK DINAMO Ltd. operates as the Group market company, managing commercial placement, end-customer relationships and market risk.

Why these projects exist
