The portfolio connects technology, capital, markets, knowledge and operational delivery. Each project is shown according to its actual stage and is not treated as completed without documented results, studies and approvals.

Role of GNK ASG d.o.o.

Development integrator and IP assembler of the Group

GNK ASG d.o.o. integrates partial code, modules, libraries, connectors and other development inputs into deployable software solutions, while providing quality assurance, documentation, configuration and handover support.

GNK DINAMO Ltd. operates as the Group market company, managing commercial placement, end-customer relationships and market risk.
