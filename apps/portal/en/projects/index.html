Nine actual projects. No web modules presented as investments.

The public register presents the objective, phase and next permitted step. Planned, feasibility, review, development, due diligence and restricted disclosure are not claims of completion.

Important

Mail Studio, Finance Portal, Newsroom, Admin and other digital modules are not business projects. They are Digital Headquarters infrastructure.

The label next to each project (e.g. HLT-001) is a proposed internal code for the confidential company linked to that project — not an official name. The name, jurisdiction and ownership structure are published only once the relevant legal processes conclude. GROUP-INT denotes GNK DINAMO Ltd. Group, which already operates publicly under that name.
