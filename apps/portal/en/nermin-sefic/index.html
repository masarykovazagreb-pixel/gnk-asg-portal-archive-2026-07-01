Who is Nermin Sefić?

Nermin Sefić is the director and founder of GNK ASG d.o.o., based in Zagreb, and the authorized representative and ultimate beneficial owner (UBO) of the parent company GNK DINAMO Ltd., based in Boulder, Colorado. He is active in finance, technology, corporate governance and market analysis, within a publicly documented group structure connecting dozens of related companies.

Publications

Nermin Sefić is the author of the book "Osnove revizije" (Adamić — Naklada Val, 2003, 119 pp.), a professional handbook on the function of auditing in everyday business, listed in the CROSBI Croatian scientific bibliography. He is a co-author of "Financije za poduzetnike i menadžere nefinancijaše" (Adamić, Rijeka, 2003), and author of the guide "Poduzetnik — Vodič za poduzetnike i one koji bi to htjeli postati" (RI TELEFAX d.o.o., Rijeka, 2006, ISBN 953-6345-26-9), also used in adult education (VIMAL), available on SlideShare. He also authored the lecture "Kontroling i suvremene korporativne financije", available on SlideShare.
