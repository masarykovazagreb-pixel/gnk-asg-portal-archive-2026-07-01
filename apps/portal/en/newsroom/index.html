Newsroom

The Group's central information and media hub

Newsroom is the single place for the latest news, official announcements, analyses, author commentaries, market information, public documents and media communication.

The platform connects business, technology, project and group news from our own announcements and verified sources. It enables users quick access to corporate information, statements, financial and project reports, market indicators and content that provides broader context on important business events.

A dedicated section of Newsroom is intended for journalists and media partners, including applications, accreditations and official contacts for media inquiries.

Every publication must include the source, date and verification status and clearly distinguish confirmed facts from estimates and future projections. This builds a transparent, verifiable and responsible public communication channel of the Group.
