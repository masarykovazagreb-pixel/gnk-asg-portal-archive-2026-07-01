GNK ASG d.o.o. processes personal data in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) and applicable Croatian data protection regulations.

The data controller is GNK ASG d.o.o., Zagrebačka cesta 130, Zagreb, Tax ID 75227917632. For data protection enquiries, contact it@gnk-asg.hr.

  • Contact form: name and surname, e-mail address, phone (optional), enquiry department, message content.
