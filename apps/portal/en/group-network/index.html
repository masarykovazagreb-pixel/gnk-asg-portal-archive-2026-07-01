The Group's global network

One network. Multiple markets. A shared direction.

The global network of the GNK DINAMO Ltd. Group connects companies, projects and operating locations into a single international business system.

Each company operates locally, in accordance with the legal, tax and market rules of the country in which it does business. At the same time, the entire Group is connected by a shared strategy, governance standards and digital infrastructure.

This model enables faster exchange of knowledge, more efficient use of capital and technology, and the transfer of successful business solutions from one market to another. Projects can be developed locally, tested under real conditions, and then adapted for use across the wider Group network.

The strength of the network comes not only from the number of companies and locations, but from how connected they are. The shared system enables coordinated project management, standardized reporting, safer execution and clearer accountability at every level of the business.

The Group's global network creates the foundation for international growth, new investments and project development in technology, finance, sport, media, mobility, real estate and digital solutions.

Each company retains its own legal and operational independence, while at the same time contributing to the shared development, stability and long-term value of the entire Group.
