Romania Downs Two Russian-Type Geran Drones Over the Black Sea

18 октомври 2026 г. · Източник: Novinite.com ↗

Резюме на източника

Romanian F-16 fighter jets shot down two jet-powered Geran-series strike drones over the country's territorial waters in the Black Sea on October 17, with the Romanian Ministry of National Defense identifying the aircraft as belonging to a type used by Russia in the war in Ukraine. The ministry said the drones were jet-powered models from the Geran family, the designation Russia uses for Iranian Shahed kamikaze drones. According to BGNES, two separate aircraft destroyed the drones within four minutes of each other. ...
