Bulgarian Authorities Seizes Nearly 39 Tons of Nitrous Oxide in Sofia

19 октомври 2026 г. · Източник: Novinite.com ↗

Резюме на източника

Nearly 38.76 tons of nitrous oxide were seized from three warehouses at the same address in Sofia during joint inspections by the Bulgarian Food Safety Agency and customs officials, the agency said. The operation was conducted by inspectors from the Sofia Regional Food Safety Directorate and representatives of the Sofia Customs Territorial Directorate following a delivery of nitrous oxide, also known as laughing gas. ...
