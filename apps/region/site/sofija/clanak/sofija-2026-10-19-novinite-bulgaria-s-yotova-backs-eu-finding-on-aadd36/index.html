Bulgaria's Yotova Backs EU Finding on Russia’s Role in Black Sea Strikes

19 октомври 2026 г. · Източник: Novinite.com ↗

Резюме на източника

President Iliana Yotova told Nova TV that she accepts the European Council’s conclusion that Russia is responsible for strikes in the Black Sea, but said Bulgaria must await evidence from the investigation before taking diplomatic action, while stressing that the incidents do not constitute a threat to national security. “There are certainly risks and threats to safety in the Black Sea. I cannot speak of a threat to national security,” Yotova said. ...
