Bulgaria Targets 3% Deficit as 2027 Budget Debate Exposes Divisions

19 октомври 2026 г. · Източник: Novinite.com ↗

Резюме на източника

Bulgaria will seek to keep its budget deficit within the 3% limit, Deputy Prime Minister Ivo Hristov said on BNT, adding that the final budget figures would depend on parliamentary discussions and subsequent revisions. Hristov also discussed plans to raise pensions by 8%, public sector salaries by 5%, and teachers’ pay. He argued that the increases should prioritize those most affected by rising costs rather than apply the same approach across the board. ...
