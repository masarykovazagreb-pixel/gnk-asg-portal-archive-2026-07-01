Network notice. This is an independent edition inside the GNK ASG network. The main site is gnk-asg.hr; the list of all editions is at gnk-asg.hr/en/network. The publisher is AKTUAL MEDIA d.o.o., and every text in the network is listed at AKTUAL MEDIA — network of editions. Editor and director: Nermin Sefić.

This is not medical advice. The texts explain public-health guidance and how to read a study; decisions about treatment need a physician.

01Checks

Figures come from guidelines and reviews, not from single studies without context. When guidelines differ, we say so — the difference is information.

02What we do not do

We do not diagnose, recommend treatment, sell supplements, or publish recovery stories without documentation.

  • Guideline before a single study
  • Absolute risk always with relative
  • No diagnoses or treatment
  • Corrections in the same place

Questions that keep coming back

Why no patient names?

Because health data belongs to the person, not the outlet. We write about populations and procedures, not individual cases without consent.

Sources

Every source below is public and checkable. Where a page cites a figure, the figure comes from one of these documents — and the document has a date.

  1. World Health OrganizationGuidelines on physical activity, nutrition and prevention.

  4. NHSPractical patient guidance, clearly dated.
