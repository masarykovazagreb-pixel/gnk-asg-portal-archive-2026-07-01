How SCENA ZNANOSTI is made

One rule: the genre is never hidden. A preprint is a preprint, a reviewed paper is a reviewed paper, and a press release is a press release.

Network notice. This is an independent edition inside the GNK ASG network. The main site is gnk-asg.hr; the list of all editions is at gnk-asg.hr/en/network. Editor and director: Nermin Sefić.

The desk does not run research or peer-review papers. It carries published documents and marks what is peer-reviewed and what is not.

01Checks before publishing

We check that the paper exists, whether it was reviewed, what the sample is, and whether it was later corrected or retracted. If it was retracted, that is part of the story, not a detail to erase.

02What we do not do

We do not translate press releases as results, we do not publish numbers without a source, and we do not use the word “proven” for a single study.

  • Genre always stated
  • Sample always stated
  • Retractions are not erased
  • Corrections in the same place

Questions that keep coming back

Do you write about your own preferences?

No. The desk follows the process; it does not advocate a research direction.
