Outlets the desk reads

The desk does not pick outlets by sympathy. The archive carries whatever the eight city editions ingest, and public feeds are read from a fixed list. Both lists are public and change when the source does.

02How to check an item

Every item links to the original. If a headline later changes, the archive keeps the one captured at ingestion — and that is the only version this desk can vouch for.

Sources

Every source below is public and checkable. Where a page cites a figure, the figure comes from one of these documents — and the document has a date.

  1. DanasA Belgrade daily; covers election topics in its politics section.
  2. N1 SrbijaTelevision and portal; publishes announcements, statements and election analysis.
  3. Nova.rsBelgrade portal; follows the campaign, party rallies and local races.
  4. Jutarnji listZagreb daily; covers the Serbian election from a Croatian perspective.
  5. N1 BiHN1’s Sarajevo newsroom; follows the links between Serbia’s election and Bosnia.
  6. BukaAn outlet whose items the desk recorded during the observed period.
  7. AKTUAL MEDIA — arhiva gradskih izdanjaEight city editions and their archive; the only source of the figures on this desk.
