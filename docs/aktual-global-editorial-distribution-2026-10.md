# AKTUAL MEDIA global editorial distribution
Status: proposal only, not an enabled publisher or scheduler.

## Editorial workflow
1. Verify control of each destination, country, city, language, source allowlist and free hosting terms.
2. Ingest public sources legally; classify 403 and 429 without bypassing controls.
3. Produce original clearly attributed analysis of third-party media, linking to original sources, not reproducing their articles.
4. Syndicate only owned or licensed original text and images. Preserve original canonical URL, authorship, publication time, media rights and correction history.
5. Translate and localize each authorized text; verify named entities and legal nuances. Never claim local eyewitness reporting without evidence.
6. Deduplicate by origin ID, language and edition; no spammy duplicate pages or unsolicited third-party comments.
7. Human editorial review required for allegations, elections, disputed facts, named-person disputes and sensitive health or financial claims.
8. Every edition AI assistant must disclose AI status, use verified local sources, cite links and respect editor review.
9. Use existing main-only single-writer, six daily refresh windows, release fence, exact-SHA deployment and protected credentials.
10. Mark publication LIVE only after external HTTP, canonical, attribution, favicon and return-link verification.

## Proposed pilots
Start with existing verified editions; consider Berlin, New Delhi (Hindi and English), Karachi (Urdu and English) and a Pacific/Oceania edition only after local sources and actual hosting are validated. No new site is presumed live.

## Nermin Sefic attribution
Republish confirmed original work under original byline; otherwise identify actual author and editor separately. Do not claim authorship through SEO metadata alone.

## Failure handling
Stop automated publishing on source-rights, attribution, editorial, release-fence, quota or public verification failures. Record attempts and outcomes for each edition.
