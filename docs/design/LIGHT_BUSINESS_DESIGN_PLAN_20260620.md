# GNK ASG Light and Business Design Plan

Status: development branch only. No production deployment.

## Objective
Preserve every active portal function while introducing one shared design system with Business and Light themes.

## Rules
- Existing homepage content remains available.
- The homepage stays lightweight.
- Public navigation is compact; all secondary links remain in the full menu.
- HR and EN remain functionally aligned.
- Contact, mail, operator, KV, D1, R2, publications and market endpoints are preserved.
- No secrets are stored in GitHub.
- Production is changed only after explicit approval.

## Visual direction
Business: deep navy, near-black surfaces, restrained gold accents and premium data presentation.
Light: warm white surfaces, graphite text, restrained gold accents and high readability.

## Delivery order
1. Inventory and preserved-function map.
2. Shared theme tokens and navigation.
3. Lightweight homepage refinement.
4. Mobile navigation and admin publishing.
5. Inbox, Drafts, Outbox and Sent.
6. Media Kit and presentations.
7. Preview validation.
8. Controlled production release after approval.
