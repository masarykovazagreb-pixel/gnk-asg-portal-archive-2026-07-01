## Blogger — project note

**Title:** Project Adria Bank: development with permissions pending

GNK ASG is developing Project Adria Bank while preparing a group-level system. According to the project sponsor, relevant documentation has been submitted to the competent authorities; required permissions remain pending. Filing is not an authorisation, and no banking or financial service is currently offered through the project.

The public desktop experience is a local, synthetic demo for exploring simulated customer and staff workflows. It does not connect to banks, payment networks, card processors, exchanges or regulators, and it does not open real accounts or move funds. Do not use real personal, banking, identity, card or business data.

The project has an indicative objective to prepare a first potential market entry outside Croatia within 14 months of 12 October 2026 (by 12 December 2027). This is a conditional planning objective, not a promised opening date. It depends on authorisations, the selected legal entity and country, capital, governance, security and operational readiness. The current market list is for research, not a confirmed launch schedule.

## Dev.to — technology-focused version

**Title:** Designing a local banking-system demo while regulatory work continues

Project Adria Bank separates system development from permission to provide financial services. GNK ASG says relevant documents have been submitted and that required permissions remain pending. In parallel, the group is developing a local simulator for synthetic accounts, maker/checker steps, virtual/prepaid-card lifecycle rehearsal, daily reference-rate display and interoperability exercises.

The simulator is not connected to a bank, payment rail, card processor, exchange or regulator. Its account records, values, adapters and workflows are fictional fixtures. A passing format check or simulated approval is not proof of a real account, institution, licence or transaction.

The project's 14-month objective is conditional: it concerns preparing a potential first step outside Croatia, not a guaranteed opening or a list of authorised markets. Country selection and scope depend on regulatory decisions and operational readiness.
